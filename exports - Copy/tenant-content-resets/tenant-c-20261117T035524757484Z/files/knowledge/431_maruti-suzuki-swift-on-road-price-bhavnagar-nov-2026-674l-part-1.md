## Maruti Suzuki Swift On Road Price in Bhavnagar - Starts at ₹6.74 Lakh

Updated on November 2026

The Maruti Suzuki Swift on-road price in Bhavnagar starts at ₹6,73,623 for the base LXi variant, which includes an ex-showroom price of ₹5,83,852, RTO charges of ₹56,131, and insurance of ₹33,640. The top-spec ZXi Plus AMT DT variant is priced at ₹10,14,034 on-road. Maruti Suzuki offers 17 Swift variants across petrol and CNG, with manual and automatic transmission options.
