# Service Schedule by Purchase Date

When an owner asks "Which service does my car need? I purchased it on <date>", collect the delivery date and current odometer reading (km). Apply whichever threshold arrives earlier—months elapsed or kilometres driven—using the schedule below.

| Time Since Delivery | Typical Odometer | Service Required | Scope of Work |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| About 1 month | ~1,000 km | 1st Complimentary Inspection | Fluid levels, tyre pressures, underbody fastener check, ECU software scan |
| About 6 months | ~10,000 km | 1st Periodic Maintenance | Synthetic engine oil + OEM oil filter, 40-point checklist, washer & coolant top-up |
| About 12 months | ~20,000 km | 2nd Periodic Maintenance | Oil & filter, engine air filter & cabin pollen filter replacement, brake pad check |
| About 18 months | ~30,000 km | 3rd Periodic Maintenance | Oil & filter, wheel alignment & balancing, battery health & suspension check |
| About 24 months | ~40,000 km | 4th Major Maintenance | Full oil service + brake fluid flush, coolant renewal, spark plugs / fuel filter as due |
| Every 6 months thereafter | Every ~10,000 km | Ongoing Periodic Service | Scheduled oil & filter service; inspect drive belts, tyres & wear items |
| Any warning light / impact | Any km | Diagnostic / Repair Visit | OBD fault scan, itemized repair estimate, mechanical or body-shop work |

**Calculation rule:** Compare months elapsed since delivery against current odometer reading. For example, a car delivered on 10 March 2026 checked on 10 November 2026 (~8 months) with 11,500 km has crossed both the 6-month and 10,000 km marks and is due for its 1st Periodic Maintenance. If only the purchase date is shared, guide using the time column and ask for exact km at booking.
