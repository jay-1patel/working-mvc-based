## Reise Traverse All-Terrain Suit Review | Comfort, Protection and Touring Practicality

- Published November 15, 2026
- Views : 322
- 9 min read

Adventure riding asks a lot from your gear. It has to protect you when things go wrong, keep you comfortable when you're spending hours in the saddle, and be practical enough to live with when the ride takes you far from home.

The Reise Traverse All-Terrain Suit is designed to do all three. It combines a touring jacket and pant with CE Level 2 D3O armour, abrasion-resistant materials, adjustable ventilation and a host of features aimed at making long-distance riding easier.

But a long list of features doesn't always tell you what a suit is like to use. So we put the Traverse through a series of simple tests and looked at it across three areas that matter most: comfort, mobility and protection, and touring practicality.
