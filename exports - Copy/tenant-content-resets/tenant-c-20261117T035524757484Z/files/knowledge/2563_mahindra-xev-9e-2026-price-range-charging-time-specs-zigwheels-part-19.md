## Verdict

**Who Should Buy The Mahindra XEV 9e?**

- The XEV 9e is an electric SUV that makes for a sensible style statement.
- While it looks like a lifestyle SUV, at its core it’s a comfortable, spacious and practical car for the family
- The cherry on top is the tech package that’s more elaborate and loaded than what some luxury SUVs offer while charging more than double the price
- Additionally, the performance is as exciting as it is usable with battery pack options that’ll manage a real-world range of 400-550km quite easily
- The Mahindra XEV 9e is an SUV truly built to global standards. What remains to be seen is if the full range of variants is still offered at a reasonably India-friendly price
