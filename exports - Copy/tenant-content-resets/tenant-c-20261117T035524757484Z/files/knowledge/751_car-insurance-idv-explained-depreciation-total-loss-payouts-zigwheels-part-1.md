One flooded basement, one highway pile-up, one electrical fire. That is all it takes for a car you have loved for years to turn into a claim file. And in that moment, the single number that decides whether you walk away with a new set of keys or a hole in your savings is one most owners never look at during renewal: the car's insured value.

In India, that number is called the Insured Declared Value, or IDV. It quietly sets the ceiling on what your insurer pays for severe damage and shapes whether your car gets fixed, written off, or replaced. Here is how it works, and why getting it right matters far more than saving a few hundred rupees on premium.
