TVS launched two new shades for the Jupiter 125 - Ivory Matte Copper Bronze and Ivory Elite Green.

26 May, 2026

Deepanshi Chaudhary

These new colours are added to the DT SXC variant, which now has four colour options, including Ivory brown and Ivory Grey.

The Ivory Matte Copper Bronze gets a copper-coloured apron, front fender and side panels and white colour on the sides of the apron, floorboard, flyscreen and above the tail light.

The scooter gets a tri-tone finish with a dark brown seat and brown inner panels. This combination looks premium and pleasing.

The Ivory Elite Green has pastel green colour on the apron, front fender and side panels, and the remaining panels get ivory shade with the same dark brown seat and brown inner panels.

In the green colour, the scooter looks subtle and has a fresh, calming vibe.

The Jupiter 125 just gets a cosmetic update and doesn't see any other change.
