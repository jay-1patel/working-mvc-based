## About Yamaha

Yamaha’s Indian journey began in 1983, when Escorts started licence production of the Yamaha RD350, and in 1985 Yamaha signed a technical agreement with the Escorts group to start local production of Yamaha motorcycles in the country. In 1995 the two formed a 50:50 joint venture, Escorts Yamaha Motor Ltd. Under this partnership, Escorts Yamaha produced some iconic motorcycles such as the RD350 (sold as the Rajdoot 350) and the RX100, all of which tickled the fancies of performance motorcycle enthusiasts in India. Yamaha made many successful 2-stroke motorcycles in India such as the RD, RX and RXG, but with the growing demand for more fuel-efficient motorcycles and ever more stringent emissions norms, the company made their first 4-stroke for India - the YBX 125.
