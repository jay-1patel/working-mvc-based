## About Tata

Tata Motors is currently the largest electric vehicle manufacturer with close to 40 percent market share. Tata has shown great zeal towards electrification of its portfolio. Tata Motors is selling multiple electric cars, including the Tiago EV, Tigor EV, Punch EV, Nexon EV, Curvv EV and the Harrier EV. Tata Motors plans to further expand its EV portfolio, which will include the Altroz EV, Sierra EV and Safari EV.
