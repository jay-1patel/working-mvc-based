## About Kia

**History**

Korean automaker Kia has a long history of producing global cars for sale in Europe and other countries. It started out in 1944 as a bicycle manufacturer and graduated to producing cars in the 1980s. Since then, it has become a globally renowned carmaker under its parent company, Hyundai.

Kia Motors set up a manufacturing facility to build cars in India in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh, in 2017. It has been in India for barely seven years, but already, it's a mainstream carmaker with multiple models on sale. The Seltos was Kia's debut car in 2019, based on the SP2 SUV concept that was globally unveiled in India at Auto Expo 2018. It was launched in the lucrative compact SUV segment that allowed Kia to price the car well in India. Since then, the automaker has introduced vehicles at multiple price points – a full-size people carrier, a more affordable alternative to the Seltos, the smaller Sonet SUV, the Syros subcompact SUV, and a smaller MPV.
