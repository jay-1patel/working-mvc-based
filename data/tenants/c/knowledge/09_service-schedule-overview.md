# MetroDrive Autos Service Schedule

## 1st Periodic / Major Check
- **Timing:** About 24 months or ~40,000 km (depending on chart)
- **Includes:** Oil service, spark plugs, coolant, brake fluid

## Ongoing Periodic Service
- **Frequency:** Every 6 months or ~10,000 km
- **Includes:** Repeat oil service, replace filters and wear parts when due

## Repair / Diagnosis
- **Trigger:** Any time after accident or warning light
- **Includes:** Scan, estimate, denting-painting, or mechanical repair

> **Note:** Always confirm a written estimate before work begins.
