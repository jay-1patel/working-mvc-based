## About MG

MG Motor, also known as Morris Garages, made its India debut in 2019 with the Hector midsize SUV. The MG Hector quickly reaped success as it’s a spacious 5-seater SUV with a long list of features. It continues to be the best seller in MG’s India lineup followed by the green ZS EV electric SUV. MG predominantly has an SUV lineup in the country. But it’ll soon launch the G10 premium MPV which recently made its debut at Auto Expo 2020. MG Motor India has an ambitious plan in the Indian market and will soon expand its portfolio. Its upcoming vehicles include the G10 premium MPV, Hector Plus 6-/7-seater SUV and the Gloster full-size SUV. MG cars are known for their long list of features. It is one manufacturer which brought connected car technology and online features to mainstream cars. If there’s one thing that’s missing from MG India’s portfolio, that’ll be some fun sedans and crossovers.
