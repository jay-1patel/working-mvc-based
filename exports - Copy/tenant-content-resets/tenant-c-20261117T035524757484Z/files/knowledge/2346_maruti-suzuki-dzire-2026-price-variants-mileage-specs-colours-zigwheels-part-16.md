## Performance & Mileage

**Will I Like Driving The New Dzire?**

- 1.2-litre, three-cylinder engine makes 82PS and 112Nm. Available in petrol and CNG.
- Transmission options: 5-speed MT and 5-speed AMT.
- Very easy to drive. Does not require frequent gear changes in the city.
- Comfortably maintains 80-100kmph on the highway.
- Noise and vibration levels could be controlled better.
- AMT isn’t very smooth, upshifts a little too early.
