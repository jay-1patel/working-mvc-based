## Features

**Feature Loaded To Its Gills**

- Massive 15.6-inch touchscreen that is bright and presents good colours and supports wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay.
- Smaller 8.8-inch digital driver’s display with multiple display layouts but no support for navigation on it.
- Excellent 9-speaker Infinity sound system that gives a bass-heavy audio experience.
- Other highlights: Panoramic glass roof, 64-colour ambient lighting, 6-way power adjustable driver’s seat and ventilated front seats.
- The 'Pro Variant also gets powered tailgate as well as Level-2 ADAS
- Most of the controls are on the touchscreen which take some time getting used to.
