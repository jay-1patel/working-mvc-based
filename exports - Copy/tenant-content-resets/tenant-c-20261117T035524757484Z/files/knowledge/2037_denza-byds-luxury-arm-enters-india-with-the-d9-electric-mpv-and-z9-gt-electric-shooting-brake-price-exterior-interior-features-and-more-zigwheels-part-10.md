## Exterior

The Z9 GT is unlike anything that you have seen in India. It comes in a unique new body style, featuring a sporty silhouette. At the front, the Z9 GT gets split LED headlamps with 108 lighting zones, a ridged bonnet and discreet air intakes on the bumper. A LiDAR sensor sits on the leading edge of the roof.

It measures around 5,180mm in length, 1,990mm in width and 1,490mm in height, with a 3,125mm wheelbase. It sits on 20-inch alloy wheels, a prominent character line, flush door handles for a premium effect, and the Denza logo on the D-pillars.

At the rear, you get a dual-ridge roof spoiler with a stop lamp in the centre, a heavily raked windscreen, wide LED tail-lamps and a layered bumper. A cool feature is that even the rear wheels steer by up to 15 degrees, which allows the Z9 GT to ‘crab walk’ and perform 360-degree turns.
