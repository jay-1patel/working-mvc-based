A car’s paint does not always tell the truth, even when the seller is trying to. A paint thickness gauge is one of the most useful tools you can carry, and a decent one costs less than filling up a tank of fuel. Factory paint stays within a fairly tight thickness range across the entire car. Paint consistency should be checked here; that is, if one door or a quarter panel reads noticeably different from the rest, that panel has been repainted.
