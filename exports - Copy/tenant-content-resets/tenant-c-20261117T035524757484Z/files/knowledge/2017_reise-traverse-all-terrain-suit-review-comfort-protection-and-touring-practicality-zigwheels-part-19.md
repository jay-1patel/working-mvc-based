## The Verdict

The Reise Traverse All-Terrain Suit brings together a comprehensive set of features for adventure and touring riders. The CE Level 2 D3O armour covers the major impact zones, while Cordura, SuperFabric and ripstop construction provide reinforcement across key areas. The four-way stretch panels, AirMorph ventilation system and multiple adjustment points address the demands of all-day riding, while the storage, suspenders, boot compatibility and 360-degree jacket-pant connection make it a more complete touring setup.

What matters, though, is how all these elements work together. Protection is important, but so is being able to move freely. Ventilation matters, but so does the fit. And a suit designed for long-distance riding needs to be practical enough to use day after day.

The Protection Detail Lab gives us a way to look at these features more closely, but the real measure of a touring suit is how well it works once you leave the studio and get out on the road.
