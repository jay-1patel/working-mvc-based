## Mobility

We started with a simple question. How easy is it to move around in the Traverse?

We tried squatting, walking, climbing, getting on and off the motorcycle, and moving between seated and standing riding positions. These are all things you end up doing on an adventure ride, whether you're negotiating a difficult section of trail or simply stopping for a break.

The Traverse uses four-way stretch panels and multiple adjustment points to help with movement. These allow the jacket and pants to accommodate changes in riding position without making the suit feel unnecessarily restrictive.

The fit is particularly important around the shoulders, elbows, waist and knees. These are the areas that do most of the work when you're moving around on a motorcycle, and they need enough room to do so comfortably.

The idea here is simple. Riding gear shouldn't make it difficult to move around on the bike it's supposed to be used on.
