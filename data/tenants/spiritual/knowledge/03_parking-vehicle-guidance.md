# Parking & Vehicle Guidance

The temple lane (Har Ki Pauri Road) is a no-vehicle walking lane. All vehicles park at the Divine Guide Parking, 300 metres before Gate 1 — a flat, lit walking path connects the parking to the temple.

| Vehicle type | Parking charge | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Two-wheeler (bike / scooter) | Rs. 20 / day | Helmet locker available free |
| Car / jeep | Rs. 50 / day | About 120 car spaces; fills by 9 AM on weekends |
| Tempo traveller / mini bus | Rs. 150 / day | Park in the upper bay; inform the office for group darshan |
| Bus | Rs. 200 / day | Upper bay only; overnight parking with office permission |
| Bicycle | Free | Stand near the two-wheeler bay |

**Good to know:**
*   Parking is open 4:30 AM – 10:00 PM, with a guard at night for permitted vehicles.
*   EV charging (2 points) is available in the car bay at Rs. 150 per charge.
*   Autos and e-rickshaws drop passengers at the lane entrance, 300 metres from the temple — they cannot enter the walking lane.
*   During Kumbh / Kanwar Yatra and on Mahashivratri, parking shifts to the government grounds at Bairagi Camp (about 2 km) with free shuttle e-rickshaws to the lane entrance.
*   The temple does not charge anything for parking of vehicles bringing devotees with disabilities — show the disability certificate at the parking gate.
