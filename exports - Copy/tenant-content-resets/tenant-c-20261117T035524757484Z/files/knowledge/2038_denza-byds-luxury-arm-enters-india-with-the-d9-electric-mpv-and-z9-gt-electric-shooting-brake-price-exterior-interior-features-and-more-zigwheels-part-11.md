## Interior

The Z9 GT is a five-seater, and the dashboard is dominated by a triple-screen layout and a dual-tone cabin scheme. You also get a screen on each front door. On cars with camera-based mirrors, these show the camera feed, while on cars with conventional mirrors, they display a pattern synced with the ambient lighting.

The centre console gets multiple storage spaces for your mobile phones and cups, and there are also physical buttons for certain controls. It comes with a three-spoke steering wheel and well-bolstered seats.

Overall, the interior is a fun yet comfortable place to be in, with plenty of displays and a minimalist approach that looks clean.
