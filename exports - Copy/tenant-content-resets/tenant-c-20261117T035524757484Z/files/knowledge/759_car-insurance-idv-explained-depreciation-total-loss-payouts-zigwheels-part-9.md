Declare every accessory; non-factory fitted accessories count toward IDV only if you declare them separately. The insurer can’t see the touchscreen, alloy set, or CNG kit unit on the policy.

Use the flexibility you're given. Insurers let you set IDV within an approved band at purchase or renewal. Picking the realistic upper end instead of the bargain-basement floor keeps your payout honest.

Re-check when your car changes brackets. Moving from year two to year three bumps IDV depreciation from 20 percent to 30 percent, so that renewal deserves a proper look, not a lazy auto-renew.

Match add-ons to your car's age. Return to Invoice guards new cars against the IDV gap, while zero depreciation protects repair bills on partial damage.
