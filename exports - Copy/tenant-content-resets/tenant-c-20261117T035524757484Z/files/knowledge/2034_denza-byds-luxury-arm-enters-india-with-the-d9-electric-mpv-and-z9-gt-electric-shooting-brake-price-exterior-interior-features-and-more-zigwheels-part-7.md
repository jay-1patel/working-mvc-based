## Features And Safety

The dashboard houses a 15.6-inch touchscreen, a 10.25-inch digital driver’s display, a 16-speaker Devialet sound system, a panoramic sunroof, 3-zone climate control and ambient lighting.

Safety is taken care of by an ADAS suite, nine airbags, an electronic parking brake (EPB), a 360-degree camera with blind-spot monitor, front and rear parking sensors, ISOFIX child seat mounts, multiple parking sensors and a tyre-pressure monitoring system (TPMS).
