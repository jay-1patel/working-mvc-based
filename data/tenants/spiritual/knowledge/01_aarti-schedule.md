# Daily Aarti Schedule

| Aarti | Time | Duration | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mangala Aarti | 5:00 AM | 20 min | Temple opens with this aarti; the most peaceful darshan of the day |
| Shringar Aarti | 8:00 AM | 15 min | After the morning abhishek and shringar |
| Bhog Aarti | 12:00 PM | 15 min | Followed by darshan closing at 1:00 PM |
| Sandhya Aarti (Ganga Aarti) | 6:30 PM | 30 min | The main aarti, held at the ghat steps — arrive by 6:00 PM for a place |
| Shayan Aarti | 8:45 PM | 10 min | Temple closes after this aarti |

**Season note:** In winter (November–February) the Sandhya Aarti is at 6:00 PM and Shayan Aarti at 8:15 PM. All other aartis stay the same.

**For devotees attending the aarti:**
*   Darshan pauses during each aarti for 10–15 minutes; the queue resumes right after.
*   The Sandhya Aarti is best seen from the ghat steps opposite the sanctum. Sitting space is free; arrive 30 minutes early on weekends and festival days.
*   One family per day can be the Aarti Yajman (Rs. 501, book 2 days ahead at the seva desk).
*   Aarti is also shown on the screen in the annakshetra hall for elderly devotees who cannot stand in the crowd.
