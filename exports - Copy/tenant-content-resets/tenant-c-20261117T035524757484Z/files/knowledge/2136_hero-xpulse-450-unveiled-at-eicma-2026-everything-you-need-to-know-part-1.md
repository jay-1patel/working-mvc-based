Hero XPulse 450 Unveiled at EICMA 2026: Everything You Need to Know

by Jehan Adil Darukhanawala

November 11, 2026

View : 48

Hero finally pulled the covers off the XPulse 450 at EICMA 2026, and this is not a small update to the existing lineup. The bike steps into proper mid-displacement adventure territory with a new 450cc engine, a trellis frame, and a design that looks built for more than just weekend trails. This walkaround covers every detail shown on the floor at Milan, from the chassis architecture and suspension setup to the electronics package and the features Hero has confirmed so far. The company claims the XPulse 450 is the most capable machine they have ever built for off-road use, though real-world performance numbers and pricing are still to be officially announced for the Indian market. What Hero has shown here is ambitious on paper. Whether the execution holds up when it reaches production and actual roads is a question worth asking.
