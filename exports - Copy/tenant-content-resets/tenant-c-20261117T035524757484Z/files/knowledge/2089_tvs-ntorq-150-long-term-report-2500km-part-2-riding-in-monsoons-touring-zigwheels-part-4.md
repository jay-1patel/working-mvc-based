## The Farewell

It’s time to bid farewell to Ntorq draws near. It’s that scooter, or in fact, a two-wheeler, that I will never mind swinging a leg over – irrespective of where I need to go, or what I need to do. While it keeps me away from my Duke, it ensures I have as much fun. Maybe I should check with my wife if her current scoot needs an upgrade, because I know the right one.
