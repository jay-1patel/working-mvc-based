# Emergency Response: Tyre Puncture or Blowout

## Symptoms
- **Blowout:** Loud bang, car suddenly pulls to one side, steering shakes hard.
- **Puncture:** Car feels soft or wobbly on one side, steering pulls, or tyre is flat when parked.
- **TPMS:** The tyre pressure warning light may come ON.

## Immediate Actions (Do This Now)
1. **If a tyre bursts:** Grip the steering wheel firmly with both hands and keep the car straight.
2. **Braking:** Do NOT brake hard. Lift your foot off the accelerator slowly and let the car slow down on its own.
3. **Lights:** Switch ON the hazard lights.
4. **Stopping:** When the car is slow, steer gently to the left side of the road and stop on a flat, firm, safe surface.
5. **After Stop:** Pull the handbrake on and switch the engine off. Get out only when it is safe, and place the warning triangle, if you have one, a few metres behind the car.

## Changing to the Spare Wheel
*Only in a safe place, and only if you feel confident.*

1. **Preparation:** Park on a flat, firm surface away from traffic, with the handbrake ON. In an automatic car keep the lever in Park; in a manual car, put it in first gear. Put a stone or brick behind the opposite wheel so the car cannot roll.
2. **Tools:** Take out the spare wheel, the jack and the wheel spanner from the boot.
3. **Loosening Nuts:** Loosen the wheel nuts of the flat tyre slightly — about half a turn each — while the wheel is still on the ground. Do not remove them yet.
