# Service Schedule Guide

## First Service
- **Timing:** ~1 month or 1,000 km (whichever earlier)
- **Cost:** Free

## Subsequent Services
- **Frequency:** Every 6 months or 10,000 km (whichever earlier)
- **Estimated Cost (Midsize Sedan):** Starts from Rs. 4,800 (labour + basic parts)

## How to Book
1. Choose 'Book Service' in the menu
2. Email service@metrodriveautos.com
3. Call +91 98250 22011

**Required Info:** Registration number, model, odometer reading, purchase date, and preferred service date.
