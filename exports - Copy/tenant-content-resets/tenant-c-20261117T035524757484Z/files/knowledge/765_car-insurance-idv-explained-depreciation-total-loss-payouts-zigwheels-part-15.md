An undervalued IDV hurts more. With so much value packed into the battery and power electronics, a low IDV leaves an even wider hole between your payout and the cost of a replacement.

Standard cover can miss EV bits. Standard policies usually don't cover home charging units and cables, and they cover battery damage only when caused by an insured event, not gradual wear.

| **Factor** | **Petrol or diesel car** | **Electric car** |
| Single most expensive component | Engine and gearbox | Battery pack, the costliest part of an EV |
| Typical major-damage risk | Engine hydrolock in floods, body damage | Battery and electrical damage from water ingress or underbody impact |
| Chance of a repair bill crossing 75 percent of IDV | Lower, spread across many parts | Higher, concentrated in the battery |
| Extra cover worth considering | Engine protection, zero depreciation | EV-specific add-on for battery and charger, zero depreciation, Return to Invoice |
