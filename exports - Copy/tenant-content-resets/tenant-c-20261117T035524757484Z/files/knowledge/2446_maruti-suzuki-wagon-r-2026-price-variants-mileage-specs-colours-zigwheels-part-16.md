## Wagon R Key Highlights

**Variants:**
The Maruti Suzuki Wagon R is available in four broad trims: LXi, VXi, ZXi and ZXi+. It is offered in Solid White, Silky White, Magma Grey, Gallant Red, Nutmeg Brown, Poolside Blue and Bluish Black. The Gallant Red and Magma Grey can also be had with a Bluish Black roof.

**Safety Rating:**
The Wagon R has scored a 1 star safety rating in NCAP crash tests.
