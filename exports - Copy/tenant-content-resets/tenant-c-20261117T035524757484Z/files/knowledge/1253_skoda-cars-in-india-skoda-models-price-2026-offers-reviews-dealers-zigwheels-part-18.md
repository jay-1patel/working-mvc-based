## About Skoda

After adding the Kodiaq SUV and Slavia sedan to its lineup in early 2022, Skoda has new MQB A0 platform based vehicles in the pipeline. The carmaker will likely also launch the next-generation Octavia vRS sometime in the distant future.

Kia received an overwhelming response with the launch of the EV6, and with Hyundai gearing up for the launch of the IONIQ 5 soon, Skoda might be keeping close tabs. Its all-electric Enyag iV was spotted testing in India recently, hinting towards a possible launch. It’ll be brought in as a completely-built unit (CBU) and will likely kick off the marque’s electric innings in India.
