# Eco Driving & Car Care

Keep your car efficient, cleaner, and longer-lasting with these tips:

- **Warm Up:** Avoid high RPM for the first 1–2 km after a cold start.
- **Driving Style:** Smooth throttle and early upshifts save fuel; hard acceleration and late braking waste it.
- **Tyres:** Keep pressure at the door-sticker PSI. Under-inflated tyres cut mileage and raise wear.
- **Weight:** Remove unused roof racks and heavy boot clutter.
- **AC:** Use wisely—recirculate after cabin cools; park in shade when possible.
- **Maintenance:** Follow the service schedule on time. Use recommended engine oil grade and genuine filters.
- **Idling:** Idle less than 30–60 seconds when waiting; switch off if safe/legal for longer stops.
- **Routes:** Plan routes to avoid peak congestion; steady 40–60 km/h city speeds are kinder on fuel.
- **Checks:** Check PUC, battery terminals, and coolant level monthly. Fix warning lights promptly.
- **Washing:** Wash with pH-neutral shampoo; avoid harsh chemicals on paint and plastic trims.
- **Long Trips:** Check tyre pressure cold, top up washer fluid, and carry a basic tool kit.
