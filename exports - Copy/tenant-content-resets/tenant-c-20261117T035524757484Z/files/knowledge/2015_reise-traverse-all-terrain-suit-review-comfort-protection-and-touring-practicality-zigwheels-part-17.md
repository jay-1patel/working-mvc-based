## Real-World Experience

This is where the lab tests take a back seat and actual riding becomes more important.

The suit might move well during a squat test, but how does it feel when you're standing on the pegs? The ventilation might work in front of a fan, but how useful is it once you're out on the road? And while the armour and reinforcements look reassuring, how comfortable are they after several hours in the saddle?

These are questions that are best answered by spending time in the gear.

Our real-world footage looks at the Traverse in use, from getting ready for a ride to spending time on the motorcycle and stopping along the way. It also gives us a chance to look at the suit as a complete system, rather than treating the jacket and pants as separate products.

The important things here are the fit, freedom of movement, ventilation, ease of use and how naturally the suit works with the rider's movements.
