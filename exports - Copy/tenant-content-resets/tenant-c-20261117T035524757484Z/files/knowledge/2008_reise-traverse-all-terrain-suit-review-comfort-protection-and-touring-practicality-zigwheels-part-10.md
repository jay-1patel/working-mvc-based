## Abrasion & Construction

Impact protection is only one part of the equation. The outer shell also needs to be able to deal with contact against rough surfaces.

The Traverse uses Cordura, SuperFabric reinforcement and heavy-duty ripstop construction across the jacket and pants. These materials are used to provide abrasion resistance and reinforcement in key areas.

To demonstrate this, we used rough surfaces such as concrete and gravel for a simple, controlled abrasion test. We then inspected the material and stitching up close.

This isn't a substitute for a standardised abrasion test, but it gives us a look at the materials and construction without simply reading out the specifications.

The reinforced panels are particularly worth looking at because not every part of a riding suit faces the same demands. Some areas need more reinforcement, while others need to allow for movement.
