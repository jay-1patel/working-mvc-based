## About Kia

For now, SUVs and people carriers, both lucrative segments in India, are the bread-and-butter for Kia. Electric vehicles (EVs) from Kia are currently available in the Indian market, including the EV6, EV9, and Carens Clavis EV. Kia's parent company Hyundai has announced plans to further expand its global electric car platform to our shores. As the Indian EV market gains momentum and our charging infrastructure improves, upcoming Kia electric models such as the Syros EV and EV5 are likely to be launched in India.

When the carmaker debuted with its first offering, it had the most cutting-edge safety and infotainment features in the segment. But since then, other carmakers have introduced mild hybrid and fully electric powertrains, and active safety and driver assistance tech to the segment. Given that the Korean brand offers vehicles with these features in other countries, catching up might not be that hard to do.
