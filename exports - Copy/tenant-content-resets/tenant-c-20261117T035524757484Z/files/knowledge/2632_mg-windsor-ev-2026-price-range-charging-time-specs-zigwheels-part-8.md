## Design

**Distinct But Eye-catching Design**

- Aerodynamic, egg-like shape with modern-looking connected LED DRLs and sleek LED headlights.
- Illuminated MG logo on the bonnet looks beautiful at night.
- Flush-fitting door handles and huge 18-inch alloy wheels gives it an upmarket feel.
- Does not get a rear wiper or washer even in its top variant.
- Unique road presence but fails to be as commanding as an SUV.
