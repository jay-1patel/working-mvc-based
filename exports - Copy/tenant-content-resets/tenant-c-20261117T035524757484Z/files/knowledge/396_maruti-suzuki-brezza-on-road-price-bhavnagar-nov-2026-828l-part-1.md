## Maruti Suzuki Brezza On Road Price in Bhavnagar - Starts at ₹8.28 Lakh

Updated on November 2026

The Maruti Suzuki Brezza on-road price in Bhavnagar starts at ₹8,28,283 for the base LXi Turbo variant, which includes an ex-showroom price of ₹7,49,852, RTO charges of ₹44,991, and insurance of ₹33,440. The top-spec ZXi Plus AT Dual Tone variant is priced at ₹15,28,153 on-road. Maruti Suzuki offers 19 Brezza variants across petrol and CNG, with manual and automatic transmission options.
