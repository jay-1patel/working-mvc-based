## About Maruti Suzuki

**Maruti Suzuki Dzire:** Took the market by a storm when it was first introduced in 2008. Seventeen years down the road and it's still one of the top selling sedans in the country.

**Maruti Suzuki WagonR:** First launched in India in 1999, the WagonR is a five-door hatchback and one of Maruti's most consistent long-term bestsellers.

**Maruti Suzuki Brezza:** Launched in 2016, the Brezza is one of the best-selling SUVs in the country.

**Maruti Suzuki Fronx:** Selling 18,829 units in April 2026, the Fronx registered 31 percent year-on-year growth making it one of Maruti's newest and fastest growing models
