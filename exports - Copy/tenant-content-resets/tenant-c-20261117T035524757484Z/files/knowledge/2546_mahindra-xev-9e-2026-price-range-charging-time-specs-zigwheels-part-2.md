## Mahindra XEV 9e

Updated: November 2026. Mahindra XEV 9e on-road price in Bhavnagar starts at ₹24.91 Lakh and goes up to ₹35.42 Lakh. It offers 59 kWh and 79 kWh battery packs with a claimed range of 522–656 km (MIDC/ARAI) and DC fast charging. A key highlight is a 5-star Bharat NCAP safety rating. It rivals the VinFast VF7, Tata Sierra, Kia Seltos and Maruti Suzuki e Vitara.
