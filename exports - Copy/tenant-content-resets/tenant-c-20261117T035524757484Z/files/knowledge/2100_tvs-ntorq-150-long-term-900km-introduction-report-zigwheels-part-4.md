## Not Family Friendly

Every coin has two sides. As much fun as the Ntorq is, it’s not a family scooter. While I don’t mind riding alone on it, my wife doesn’t prefer to sit behind me *(Are you sure it isn’t just your riding? – Jehan)* since the seat is rather compact. She did take it out for a short spin in our complex and found it as much fun to ride it than sitting pillion, though.
