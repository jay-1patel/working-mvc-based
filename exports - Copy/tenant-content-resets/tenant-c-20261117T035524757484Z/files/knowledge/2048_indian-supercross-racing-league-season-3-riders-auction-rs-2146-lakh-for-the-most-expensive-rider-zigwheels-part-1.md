## Indian Supercross Racing League Season 3 Riders Auction: Rs 21.46 Lakh For The Most Expensive Rider

- Published November 12, 2026
- Views : 435
- 1 min read

The Indian Supercross Racing League (ISRL) concluded its Season 3 rider auction in Pune on November 11, 2026. A total of 135 riders registered for the auction, which included 105 international and 30 Indian riders. Out of these, 42 riders were selected and auctioned across the six teams, which spent Rs 4.5 crore in total to buy the riders.

Australian rider Luke Clout got the highest bid of Rs 21.46 lakh from BigRock Motorsports. Meanwhile, Indian rider Rugved Barguje secured a Rs 6.5 lakh contract with PBG Ducati Legacy Racing, which is one of the highest bids for an Indian rider in ISRL’s history.

Another interesting signing was 16-year-old Indonesian rider Muhammad Athar Alghifari, who was picked up by Gujarat Trailblazers in the 250cc India & Asia Mix category, making him the youngest rider signed at the auction.
