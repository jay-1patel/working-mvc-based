## Interiors

**What’s The XEV 9e Like Inside?**

- The base dashboard seems to be a lift off the XUV700 but everything else in the cabin of the XEV 9e is brand new
- A major visual highlight is the triple screen setup that measures around 43 inches across
- The AC control console from the XUV700 also makes way for a piano black touch-panel with shortcuts for a few functions
- The cabin plastics feel smooth and leatherette padding has been used generously to deliver a premium experience
- Steering mounted controls often make you click the horn accidentally
- Piano black control panel under the central AC vents can be very fidgety to use as the icons for the hazard lights or 360-degree camera don’t always click on the first try
- You have to mind your head while getting in as the roofline swoops down sharply
