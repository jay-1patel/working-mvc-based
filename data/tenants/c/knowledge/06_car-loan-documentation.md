# Required Documents for Car Loan

We partner with HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, and Bajaj Finance.

**Salaried Individuals:**
*   PAN Card
*   Aadhaar Card
*   3 Months’ Salary Slips
*   6 Months’ Bank Statements
*   Cancelled Cheque

**Self-Employed:**
*   PAN Card
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Income Tax Returns (ITR)
*   6 Months’ Bank Statements
*   Cancelled Cheque

**Process:**
*   Down Payment: 10–20% (profile dependent)
*   Pre-approval Time: 24–48 hours
*   Assistance: Our desk helps complete the forms.
