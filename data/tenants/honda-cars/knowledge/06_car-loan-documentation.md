# Car Loan Documentation

To apply for a car loan, please prepare the following documents:

- **PAN Card**
- **Aadhaar Card**
- **Salary Slips:** Last 3 months (for salaried individuals)
- **ITR:** Last 2-3 years (for self-employed individuals)
- **Bank Statements:** Last 6 months
- **Cancelled Cheque**

**Process:**
- Down payment: 10–20% depending on profile
- Pre-approval time: 24–48 hours
- Our desk assists in completing the application form.

**Partner Banks:** HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Bajaj Finance
