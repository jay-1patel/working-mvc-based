## About Hyundai

Hyundai has stated that it plans on bringing multiple EVs in its Indian lineup by 2028. It already has the Creta Electric and Ioniq 5 on sale in India and is looking to bring more mass market EVs, including an Inster-based entry-level EV, to rival the likes of the Tata Punch EV. Apart from EVs, Hyundai is also planning to launch the Bayon crossover SUV, the Ioniq 6 electric sedan, and a facelift of the Creta in the near future.
