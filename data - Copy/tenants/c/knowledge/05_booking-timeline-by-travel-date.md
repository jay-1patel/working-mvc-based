# Booking Timeline by Travel Date

| When (before departure) | What is needed | Action for guest | What we do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **45+ days** | Trip idea / rough budget | Share dates, pax count, cities | Propose 1–2 itineraries & indicative quote |
| **30–45 days** | Hold hotels & vehicle | Pay advance as per invoice | Block stay/transport; start flight/visa if needed |
| **15–30 days** | Passenger IDs & details | Send names as on ID, age, ID proof | Finalise vouchers; confirm safari/tent if applicable |
| **7–14 days** | Balance payment | Clear dues on invoice | Issue hotel/transport vouchers & ticket copies |
| **3–7 days** | Pre-trip briefing | Confirm pickup point & emergency contacts | Share driver/hotel contacts & packing tips |
| **0–2 days / on trip** | Travel support | Keep voucher & ID handy | 24×7 on-trip desk for delays, changes, emergencies |
| **Any time after booking** | Change / cancel request | Write with booking reference | Apply supplier rules; share revised cost or refund slab |

**Note:** Calculate days from today to the customer’s travel date. If only the travel month is known, ask for exact dates before quoting hotel rates.
