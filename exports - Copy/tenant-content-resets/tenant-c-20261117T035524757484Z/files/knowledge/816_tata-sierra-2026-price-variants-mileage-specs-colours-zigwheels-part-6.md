## Sierra Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Stunning (Red Dot award-winning) design and road presence. Grabs attention even before you know it’s called Sierra.
- Good range of engines and transmissions to choose from. Turbo-petrol is exciting!
- Top-notch safety: Up to 7 airbags, ESC, Level 2 ADAS, high-resolution 360 degree camera and more.
- Tech-bomb! Triple screens, BIG panoramic sunroof, 12-speaker JBL Black sound system, front seat ventilation, and more.
- Plenty spacious and practical for the family’s use. Backseat owners will love the space, comfort and amenities.
- Big boot can easily handle road trips or airport runs.
- Great ride quality. Feels planted and ready for rough roads

**Could be Better**

- The front row is not spacious for exceptionally tall users (over 6.2-6.3ft). Most rivals in the segment are more accommodating for such drivers/passengers.
- Issues and niggles experienced such as loose trim and glitching electronics.
- Diesel engine refinement could be better
