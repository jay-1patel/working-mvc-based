And there's no undo button. Once a car is declared a total loss, its registration is cancelled, and it's off to the salvage yard. Better to get the number right before the accident than argue about it after.

Depreciation hits your claim twice, and owners often mix the two up. The first is yearly depreciation that shrinks your IDV and caps total-loss payouts. The second is depreciation on individual parts, which nibbles away at what the insurer pays for repairs. Major damage that stops just short of a write-off gets hit by the second one, and that's where it really stings.
