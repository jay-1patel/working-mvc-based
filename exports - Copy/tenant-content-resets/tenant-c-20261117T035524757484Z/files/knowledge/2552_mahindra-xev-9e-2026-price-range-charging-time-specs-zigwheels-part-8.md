## Design

**Tell Me About The XEV 9e’s Looks!**

- The XEV 9e is based on the XUV e9 concept car in its design but is based on the brand new all-electric INGLO platform
- It measures just under 4.8 metres in length and is a great example of having large dimensions helps deliver the best results for the coupe-SUV body style
- Ground clearance (unladen) stands at 207mm while its battery pack sits higher with 222mm of clearance
- Owing to its large size (94mm longer than even the XUV700), it looks best with the optional 20-inch alloy wheels but is also offered with 19-inch wheels
- The front end sports vertically-stacked full LED headlights and LED DRLs that run across the entirety of the SUV’s face
- Towards the side, you will notice the flush-fitting front door handles and hidden rear door handles.
