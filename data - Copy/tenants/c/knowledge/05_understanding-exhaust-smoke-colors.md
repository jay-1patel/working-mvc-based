# Exhaust Smoke Diagnosis

## White Smoke
- **Thin/Vapour**: Normal water vapour on cold mornings. Disappears quickly.
- **Thick/Continuous**: Serious issue. Engine burning coolant (water). Possible failed head gasket. Stop driving if overheating occurs.

## Blue/Grey-Blue Smoke
- **Meaning**: Engine burning oil.
- **Causes**: Worn piston rings or valve seals.
- **Action**: Check oil level with dipstick. Book workshop check immediately. Do not just top up oil for months.

## Black Smoke
- **Meaning**: Burning too much fuel.
- **Symptoms**: Poor mileage.
- **Causes**: Dirty air filter, injector problem, or sensor issue.
- **Action**: Drive gently, avoid hard acceleration. Book service check.
