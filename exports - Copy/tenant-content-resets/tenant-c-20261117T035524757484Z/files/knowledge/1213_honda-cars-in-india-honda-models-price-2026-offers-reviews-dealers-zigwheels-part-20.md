## About Honda

The Honda Amaze is currently available in six colour options: Platinum White Pearl, Lunar Silver Metallic, Golden Brown Metallic, Obsidian Blue Pearl, Meteoroid Grey Metallic, and Radiant Red Metallic. Unfortunately, a Golden Beige shade is not offered in the Amaze’s colour palette. As for the red option, Honda officially offers it as Radiant Red Metallic, and not Mirichi Red. For exact colour availability, we suggest checking with your nearest authorised Honda dealership, as options may vary by location and variant.
