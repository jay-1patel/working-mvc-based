## Celerio Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Responsive engine, easy to drive, comfortable low speed ride
- Spacious cabin, large boot
- Basic safety kit on all variants

**Could be Better**

- Slightly expensive
- Lack of a bigger engine option
- Bouncy high speed ride

**Expert's Conclusion**

Celerio is a well rounded package. If not for the higher price it would have become a stylish alternative to the Wagon R.
