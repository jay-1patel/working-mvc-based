## Dimensions: Big Where It Counts

- **Wheelbase (2750 mm):** The distance between the front and rear wheels. A long wheelbase gives you a stable, settled ride and the room for three proper rows.
- **Width (1917 mm):** More width means more shoulder room across each row of seats.
- **Height (1857 mm):** You sit high above traffic, with a clear view of the road ahead.
- **Boot space (786 litres):** With the third row folded, there’s room for big suitcases, camping gear or a family’s luggage for a long weekend.
- **Fuel tank (57 litres):** A large tank means longer stretches between fuel stops.
