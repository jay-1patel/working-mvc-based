## Tata Harrier EV

Updated: November 2026. Tata Harrier EV on-road price in Bhavnagar starts at ₹24.67 Lakh and goes up to ₹34.54 Lakh. It offers 65 kWh and 75 kWh battery packs with a claimed range of 538–627 km (MIDC/ARAI) and DC fast charging. A key highlight is an all-wheel-drive option. It rivals the MG Hector Tomahawk EV.
