Triumph has launched the 2027 Tiger 1200 in India in two variants, GT Pro and Rally Explorer.

31 Oct, 2026

Deepanshi Chaudhary

The GT Pro is priced at Rs 20.99 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi) and is a road-focused variant and comes in Sapphire Black and Aluminium Silver/Pacific Blue colours.

The GT Pro gets cast aluminium alloy wheels, a 20-litre fuel tank and weighs 250 kg kerb,

The Rally Explorer is priced at Rs 23.49 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi) and has off-road-focused features and hardware and comes in Ash Grey/Sapphire Black and Matt Khaki Green shades.

The Rally Explorer gets tubeless spoke wheels, a 30-litre fuel tank, Off-road Pro mode, engine guards, TPMS and 268 kg kerb weight.

It retains the 1,160 cc engine with revised internal components and gearing, and it now makes 130 Nm and 158 PS, 8 PS more than before.

This ultimate ADV gets semi-active Showa EERA heightflex suspension, which lowers the seat height by 30 mm when the bike comes to a halt.
