Honda launched the all-new CB500 in India, starting at Rs 2,75,000 (ex-showroom, Delhi).

27 Sep, 2026

Deepanshi Chaudhary

The bike is available in three variants: the base Alloy variant, the Spoke variant for Rs 2,99,900 and Spoke With Stripes at Rs 3,01,900 (all prices are ex-showroom, Delhi)

The CB500 comes in five shades: Pearl Igneous Black, Rebel Red Metallic, Mat Steel Silver Metallic, Pearl Deep Ground Gray, and Pearl Igneous Black with stripes.

The bike is powered by a 502 cc single-cylinder, air and oil-cooled engine.

This engine produces 28.1 PS and 43.3 Nm.

It gets a round LED headlight, teardrop-shaped fuel tank, single-piece seat and brushed aluminium engine casing, giving it a classic roadster look.

Underpinnings include a telescopic front fork and twin rear shock setup.

Wheels are alloys or tubeless cross-spoke units with a 19-inch front and 18-inch rear setup and disc brakes at both ends.
