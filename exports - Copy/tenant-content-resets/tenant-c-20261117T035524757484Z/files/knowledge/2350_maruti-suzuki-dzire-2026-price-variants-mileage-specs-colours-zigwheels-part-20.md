## Dzire Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Cooler design
- Spacious boot
- Still great ride quality
- 1st 5-start rated Maruti Car
- promises great mileage

**Could be Better**

- Engine noise and vibes can be felt at idle
- AMT is a bit eager to shift up for efficiency and not so smooth
- Lack of headroom in the rear seats

**Expert's Conclusion**

Still the great all-round car that it always was but now with a 5-star crash rating too! The lack of refinement from the engine and rear headroom are offset by a great feature set, efficiency, ride quality and practical use of space.
