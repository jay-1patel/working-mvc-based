## Handling

**About The Handling And Comfort**

- Steering is light to use and makes controlling this large car easy
- Handling is predictable too and it feels sharp at high speeds or while cornering
- Noise insulation is great and keeps you relaxed during long commutes
- Ride comfort is something the XEV 9e delivers in spades and even sharp potholes don’t unsettle the cabin
- While being soft enough to handle bad roads, it’s also not too soft or bouncy while driving at highway speeds
