## RWD And 4WD: The Proper SUV Layout

- **Rear-wheel drive (RWD):** The rear wheels push the SUV forward, while the front wheels only have to steer. This layout is built for heavy loads, and it’s how tough SUVs and pickups have been made for decades.
- **Shift-on-the-fly 4WD:** You can switch from 2WD to 4WD while driving, without stopping or getting out. So when the road turns muddy, you just turn a dial and keep going.
- **4-High:** For slippery surfaces at normal speeds, such as wet grass, gravel, light snow or a sandy beach road.
- **4-Low:** A dedicated low gear range for the toughest terrain, like steep climbs, deep mud and rocky trails. It multiplies the engine’s torque, so the Scorpio-N can crawl slowly and steadily over obstacles with plenty of pulling force.

**Where to find it:** 4WD is offered on the Z4 diesel MT, the Z8T diesel (MT and AT) and the Z8L 7-seater diesel (MT and AT). A Scorpio-N 4x4 starts from Rs 18.03 lakh (ex-showroom).
