## About Mahindra

SUVs: Utility vehicles dominate the Mahindra lineup, with legacy models such as the Bolero and Scorpio still on offer. The range starts with the XUV300 sub-4m SUV, and comprises the Bolero Neo, XUV700, Thar and Alturas G4 SUVs.

People movers: Legacy Mahindra models have long served as a staple in rural areas where they are still used as people carriers. Nowadays, the carmaker offers the Marazzo MPV in 6- and 7-seater layouts as a relatively premium rival to the Maruti Suzuki Ertiga.

Electric vehicle: Mahindra Electric has been developing EVs for more than two decades in India. Currently, the carmaker offers the eVerito electric sedan in India.

**Greatest Hits**

Mahindra’s top five products in India have been:

Mahindra Scorpio: first all-new global SUV from Mahindra

Mahindra Bolero: rugged utility vehicle that’s so indispensable that it’s still on sale today

Mahindra XUV500: first monocoque-based Mahindra SUV
