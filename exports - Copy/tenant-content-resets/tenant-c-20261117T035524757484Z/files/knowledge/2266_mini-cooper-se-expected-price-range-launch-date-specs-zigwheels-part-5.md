## Latest Updates on Cooper SE

The next-gen Mini Cooper SE has been previewed in new exterior images well ahead of its official unveiling. The EV features a neater, less cluttered design with curved body panels and only a few creases. It also gets a redesigned rear-end with a flatter tailgate.

The new Mini is expected to make its global debut in 2024, and its India arrival should be on the cards sometime in 2025.
