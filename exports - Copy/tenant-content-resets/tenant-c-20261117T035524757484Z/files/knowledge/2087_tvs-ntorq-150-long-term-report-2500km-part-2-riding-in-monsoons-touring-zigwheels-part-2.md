## Riding In The Rain

I am going to start with this bit since I love riding in the rain. And, long story short, the Ntorq did not disappoint. Be it commuting to the office or a weekend vadapav ride in the rain, it was a ton of fun. The scooter holds its composure on wet roads as long as you don’t ride like an idiot. Never did it make me feel like I should wait in the shade till the rain subsided, for lack of grip or because it felt scary. The only reason I considered stopping was heavy downpour with very low visibility.

And unlike on a bike, I could either dump my bag in the boot or keep it on the floorboard, depending on how heavily it was raining, to keep it dry. +1 point for convenience.
