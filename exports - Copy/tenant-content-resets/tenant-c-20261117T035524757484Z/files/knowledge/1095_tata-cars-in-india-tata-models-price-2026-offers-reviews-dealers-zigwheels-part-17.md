## About Tata

Sub-compact SUV: The Tata Nexon and Nexon EV represent Tata in one of the most competitive SUV segments in the industry where it goes up against the likes of the Kia Sonet, Maruti Brezza and Hyundai Venue.

EV: Tata ventured into the personal EV space with the Nexon EV which was then followed by the Tiago EV, Punch EV, Curvv EV and Harrier EV.

Compact SUV Coupe: Tata offers the Curvv and Curvv EV as a stylish SUV coupe that rivals the Hyundai Creta and Kia Seltos.

Mid-size SUV: With underpinnings derived from the Land Rover division, Tata offers the Tata Harrier, Harrier EV and the Tata Safari as competitors to the mid-size 5-seater SUV segment and three-row SUV space, respectively.

Sedan: Successor to the Tata Zest, Tata Tigor is a sub-4 metre sedan offered by Tata which rivals the Hyundai Aura, Maruti Dzire, and Honda Amaze.
