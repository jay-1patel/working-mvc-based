# Do's and Don'ts for Devotees

## Do
*   Take a Ganga snan before darshan if you wish — the ghat is 5 minutes away; changing rooms are available there.
*   Keep the sanctum silent and your phone on silent mode.
*   Use dustbins at both gates (plastic-free zone).

## Don't
*   Touch the Lingam or the heritage carvings — abhishek is performed by the priests on your behalf.
*   Litter — the Ganga and the temple lane are kept plastic-free.
*   Feed monkeys near the ghat steps.
*   Keep prasad bags closed while walking.
