## Space & Practicality

**Very Practical For A Family Of 5**

- Spacious interior with seating for 5 and comfortable rear seats that recline up to 135 degrees.
- Up to 604-litre boot space with adjustable floor, easily accommodates an entire suitcase set along with some smaller soft bags.
- Cabin has great storage options including multiple cup holders for all passengers and a large center console storage compartment.
- Charging sockets in the front console storage compartment are not easily accessible.
- Rear AC vent only directs airflow to one side.
