## Maruti Suzuki FRONX Running Cost

The FRONX has a running cost of Rs. 2.50 per kilometer and a monthly fuel cost of Rs. 3750, assuming a daily run of 50 kilometers and CNG at Rs. 70 per kg.

| Metric | Cost | Assumption |
|---|---|---|
| Per KM Fuel Cost | Rs. 2.50 | CNG Rs. 70/kg |
| Daily Fuel Cost | Rs. 125 | 50 km/day |
| Monthly Fuel Cost | Rs. 3750 | 1,500 km/month |
