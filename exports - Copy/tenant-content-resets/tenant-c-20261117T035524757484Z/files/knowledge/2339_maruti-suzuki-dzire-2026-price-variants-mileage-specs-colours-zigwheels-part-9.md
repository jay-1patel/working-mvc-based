## Design

- The Dzire looks low and wide despite being 10mm taller than the old car. It truly is desirable now!
- Still a very proportionate design and the remaining dimension (Length x width & wheelbase) stay the same
- Full-LED projector headlamps and DRLs grab attention
- Smoked tail lamps, dual-tone wheels and shark fin antenna give it a modern touch
- LED DRLs give it a very distinct personality
- Factory customisation options include chrome trims, puddle lamps and garnishes
- The Dzire is available in 7 colours including a black, blue and red. We don’t have a favourite yet, but th darker colours really make this new design pop!
- Fun fact: Maruti dropped the Swift badging to allow the Dzire to have its own identity. And it should, this new car now looks nothing like the Swift, at least from the outside.
- Could have offered a 16-inch alloy option as an official accessory
- 15-inch alloy wheels look small, and the tyres are narrow and look it too.
