## Powertrain

The D9 is powered by a 115.07 kWh BYD Blade battery pack with a claimed NEDC range of over 500 km.

Here are the detailed specifications:

| **Battery Pack** | **115.07 kWh** |
| Drivetrain | FWD |
| Power | 462 PS |
| Torque | 365 Nm |
| Claimed range (NEDC) | Up to 730 km |

*\*FWD: Front-wheel drive, AWD: All-wheel drive*
