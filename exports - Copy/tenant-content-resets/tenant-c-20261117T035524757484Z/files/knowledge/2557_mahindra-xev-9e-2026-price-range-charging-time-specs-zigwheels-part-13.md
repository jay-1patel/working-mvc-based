## Features

**What Features Does The Mahindra XEV 9e Offer?**

- Powered front seat with driver memory
- Ventilated front seats
- Powered tailgate
- Wireless phone charger
- Front and rear type-C USB chargers (65W)
- Fixed panoramic glass roof
- Auto AC with rear AC vents
- Multi-colour ambient lighting with animation programs
- NFC key card access
- Tilt & telescopic steering adjustment
- Drive modes
- Augmented-reality head-up display
- No full sized spare tyre, just a 135/80 R18 space saver spare tyre
