## Harrier EV Key Highlights

**Range:**
Harrier EV can go upto 622 km/charge after a full charge.

**Charging Time:**
Harrier EV battery takes 20-80 % : 25 mins to get charged.

**Variants:**
The Harrier EV is offered in three variants: Adventure, Fearless, and Empowered

The Harrier EV is offered in four colour options: Nainital Nocturne, Pure Grey, Pristine White, and Empowered Oxide.
