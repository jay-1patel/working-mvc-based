# Main Festivals at the Temple

| Festival | When | What Happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mahashivratri** | Feb–Mar | Full-night jagran, four prahar abhisheks, special prasad |
| **Shravan Mondays** | Jul–Aug | Jal abhishek all day; longest queues of the year |
| **Ganga Dussehra** | May–Jun | Special Ganga Aarti and deep daan |
| **Kartik Purnima** | Nov | Dev Deepawali — thousands of diyas at the ghat |
| **Diwali** | Oct–Nov | Deep daan after Sandhya Aarti |
| **Guru Purnima** | Jul | Special satsang in the annakshetra hall |
| **Monthly** | Ekadashi & Purnima | Special prasad and extended morning darshan |
