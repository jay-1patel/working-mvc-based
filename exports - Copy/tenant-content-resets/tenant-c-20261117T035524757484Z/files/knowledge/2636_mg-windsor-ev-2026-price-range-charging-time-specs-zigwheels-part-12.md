## Safety

**What About Safety Features?**

- 6 airbags, rear parking sensors, tyre pressure monitoring system (TPMS) and an electronic parking brake provided as standard.
- Higher variants add a 360-degree camera and rear defogger to the mix.
- 'Pro' variant offers Level-2 ADAS Tech
- 360-degree camera feed is laggy at times with odd frame drops and glitches.
