## Powertrain

The Z9 GT comes with a 122.49 kWh battery pack powering as many as three electric motors, one at the front and two at the rear, sending power to all four wheels. Here are the detailed specifications:

| **Battery Pack** | **122.49 kWh** |
| Drivetrain | AWD |
| Power | 1156 PS |
| Torque | 1210 Nm |
| 0-100 kph | 2.7 seconds |
| Claimed range (NEDC) | Up to 670 km |
