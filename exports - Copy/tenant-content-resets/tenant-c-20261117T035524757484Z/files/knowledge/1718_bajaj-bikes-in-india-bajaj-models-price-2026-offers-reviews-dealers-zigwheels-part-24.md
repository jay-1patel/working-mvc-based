## About Bajaj

**Bajaj Pulsar 220F** - when launched, it was a Karizma-killer and remains in production even now thanks to its cult following.

**Bajaj Pulsar NS200** (NS series breakout model, launched 2012) is also frequently cited as a "greatest hits" entry for introducing perimeter frames and liquid cooling to the mass-market segment

**Bajaj Chetak Electric** - launched January 2020, it revived the iconic Chetak name as an EV and has since crossed 8 lakh (800,000+) cumulative sales, making it India's second-bestselling electric scooter after TVS iQube.
