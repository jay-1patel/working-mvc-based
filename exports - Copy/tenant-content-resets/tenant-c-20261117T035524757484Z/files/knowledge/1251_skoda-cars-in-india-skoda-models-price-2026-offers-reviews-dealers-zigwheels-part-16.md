## About Skoda

History

Czech carmaker Skoda Auto has its roots tracing back to over 120 years. It started making vehicles in the 1930s, but in 1991, it became a subsidiary of the Volkswagen Group.

Skoda began its operations in India in late 2001, and launched its first car, the Octavia sedan, in 2002. It was initially imported in semi knock-down kits and assembled locally in the country. Now Skoda’s Aurangabad facility is a full-fledged manufacturing plant.

With the India 2.0 Project, the carmaker has implemented the highly localised MQB A0-IN vehicle platform. As the name suggests, it is an iteration of the global MQB A0 platform where all upcoming models will be designed and manufactured locally.

Since 2019, Skoda India has been operated under Skoda Auto Volkswagen India Private Limited, a subsidiary of the global VW Group undertaking the business activities of five brands in India: Skoda, Audi, Volkswagen, Porsche, and Lamborghini.
