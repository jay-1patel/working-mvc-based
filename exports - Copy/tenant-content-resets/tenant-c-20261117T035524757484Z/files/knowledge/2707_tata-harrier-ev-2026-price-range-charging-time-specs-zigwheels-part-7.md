## Harrier EV Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Size, road presence and similar good looks as the ICE variant, Premium interiors and a feature rich experience, Comprehensive safety along with ABS, 6-airbags and 360 degree camera

**Could be Better**

- Expect rear seat space and boot space to have reduced, Not too different looking than it’s ICE counterpart

**Expert's Conclusion**

Want a big, spacious and feature rich EV SUV, then pick the Harrier
