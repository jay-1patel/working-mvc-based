## Wheels And Tyres: Grip Meets Style

Tyre sizes can look like a random string of numbers, but each part tells you something useful. Here’s what the Scorpio-N gets:

| **Variant** | **Wheels and tyres** |
| Z2, Z4, Z6 | 245/65 R17 steel wheels |
| Z8S, Z8 | 245/65 R17 diamond-cut alloys |
| Z8T, Z8L | 255/60 R18 diamond-cut alloys (new design) |

How to read 255/60 R18:

- **255** is the tyre’s width in millimetres. Wider tyres put more rubber on the road for better grip.
- **60** is the sidewall height as a percentage of the width. Taller sidewalls cushion you over bad roads and off-road trails.
- **R18** means the tyre fits an 18-inch wheel.
- The Z8T and Z8L also get a tyre pressure monitoring system (TPMS), which shows the pressure and temperature of each tyre and alerts you if something isn’t right.
