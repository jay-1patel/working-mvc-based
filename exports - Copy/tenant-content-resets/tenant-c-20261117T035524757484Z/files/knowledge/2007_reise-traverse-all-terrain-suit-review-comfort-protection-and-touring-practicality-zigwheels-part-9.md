## The Armour Test

Jokes aside, this isn't a test of the armour's impact performance. That is what the CE certification is for. The demonstration simply gives us a way to show where the protectors sit and how the suit covers the major impact zones.

We also removed the D3O inserts to look at their shape, flexibility and construction. Flexibility matters because the armour needs to work alongside the movement of the rider rather than making the suit unnecessarily rigid.
