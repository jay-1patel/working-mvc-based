TVS recently unveiled the RTR 125 at EICMA 2026. It is the brand’s most powerful 125cc bike, and is also one of the most hi-tech. The major highlight of the RTR 125 was unquestionably its striking and sharp design, and we think the brand should consider bringing the same design language to more motorcycles in its Indian lineup. While the newer design approach could make the Apache family look more modern and aggressive, several existing models are still carrying a silhouette that has remained largely unchanged for years.

The Apache RTR 160 2V, RTR 180 2V, RTR 160 4V and RTR 200 4V have received minor styling updates over the years. However, the basic design and overall silhouette have remained more or less the same. While these motorcycles still have their own identity, a more comprehensive design update would certainly make them look fresher and more appealing.
