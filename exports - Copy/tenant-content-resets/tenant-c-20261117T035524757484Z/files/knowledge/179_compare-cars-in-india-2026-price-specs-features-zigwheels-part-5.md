The Maruti Baleno Zeta CNG is priced at Rs. 8.60 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi) and produces a maximum power of 76.43 bhp at 6000 rpm. It comes with a 1197 cc, 4-cylinder engine and delivers an ARAI-claimed mileage of 30.61 km/kg. On the other hand, the Maruti Swift ZXi CNG is priced at Rs. 8.39 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi) and generates 68.80 bhp at 5700 rpm. It is also powered by a 1197 cc engine but with a 3-cylinder setup and offers an ARAI-claimed mileage of 32.85 km/kg. Both vehicles offer a good set of features, and the choice between them largely depends on individual preferences such as design, driving experience, and mileage requirements. We would suggest taking a test drive of both vehicles to better understand their performance and comfort.
