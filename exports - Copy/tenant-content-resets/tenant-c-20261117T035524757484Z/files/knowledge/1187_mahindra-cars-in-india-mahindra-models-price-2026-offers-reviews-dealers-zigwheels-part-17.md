## About Mahindra

**History**

Mahindra is an Indian automaker that has been building cars, primarily SUVs, for the subcontinent ever since the first Willys Jeeps arrived in the 1950s. It has a legacy of building tough, diesel-powered utility vehicles, such as the CJ-series Jeeps and the Thar. Mahindra’s cars have always been popular people movers in India for the value they offer in their go-anywhere capability and price.

Four-wheel drive lies at the roots of Mahindra’s history -- it started out by assembling 4x4 Willy’s Jeeps and later developed its own off-roading vehicles and hardware such as transmissions and transfer cases. Over the years Mahindra has given us a score of memorable and capable off-roaders and family SUVs in India, at different price points across multiple segments.

**Product portfolio**

Micro SUV: Mahindra offers the KUV100 NXT micro-SUV in India. It’s a raised-up hatchback with an 82PS 1.2-litre petrol engine that rivals the Maruti Suzuki Ignis.
