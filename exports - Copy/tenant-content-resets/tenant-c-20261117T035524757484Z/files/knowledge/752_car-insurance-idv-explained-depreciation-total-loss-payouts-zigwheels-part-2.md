Here's the bit nobody reads at renewal. Your car's insured value, or IDV, is the maximum your insurer will ever pay if the car is stolen or written off. Not the showroom price you haggled over. Not what the used-car guy offers you. It's a formula-driven number that sits somewhere in between, and it shrinks every single year.

IDV is the manufacturer's listed selling price minus age-based depreciation under IRDAI norms. Aftermarket goodies count only if you declare them separately, and once the policy is issued, the number is locked in for the full term—no second chances mid-year.
