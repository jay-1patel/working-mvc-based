## Climb And Descend With Confidence

Hills and steep slopes are where many drivers feel nervous, and where Mahindra SUVs have always been at home. Every Scorpio-N, from the Z2 up, comes with two features to help on slopes.

- **Hill Hold Control:** When you stop on an uphill slope, it holds the brakes for a moment as you move your foot to the accelerator. The SUV won’t roll back.
- **Hill Descent Control:** On steep downhill slopes, it automatically limits the SUV’s speed, so you can focus on steering instead of braking.

Both are standard on every variant, which makes the Scorpio-N comfortable on ghat roads and trails, whichever variant you buy.
