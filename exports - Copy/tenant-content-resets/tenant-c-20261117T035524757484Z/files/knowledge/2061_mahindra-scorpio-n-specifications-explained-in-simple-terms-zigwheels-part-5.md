## The Engines: Mahindra Muscle, Two Ways

Mahindra SUVs have always been known for strong engines, and the Scorpio-N continues that. You get a choice of the 2-litre mStallion turbo-petrol or the 2.2-litre mHawk diesel. Both have the pull to move a full-size, three-row SUV with ease.

|  | **2-litre turbo-petrol (mStallion)** | **2.2-litre diesel (mHawk)** | **2.2-litre diesel (Z2/Z4 diesel MT)** |
| Fuel system | Turbocharged direct injection (TGDi) | Common rail direct injection | Common rail direct injection |
| Power | 203 PS @ 5000 rpm | 175 PS @ 3500 rpm | 132 PS @ 3750 rpm |
| Torque (MT) | 370 Nm @ 1750–3000 rpm | 370 Nm @ 1500–3000 rpm | 300 Nm @ 1600–2800 rpm |
| Torque (AT) | 380 Nm @ 1750–3000 rpm | 400 Nm @ 1750–2750 rpm | – |
