# How to Change a Flat Tyre

## Preparation
1. **Secure the Vehicle**: Engage handbrake. In automatic, put in Park; in manual, first gear. Place a stone/brick behind the opposite wheel.
2. **Gather Tools**: Take out spare wheel, jack, and wheel spanner from the boot.

## Steps
1. **Loosen Nuts**: Loosen wheel nuts slightly (half turn) while wheel is on ground.
2. **Jack Up**: Place jack under marked jacking point (reinforced spot near door edge). Raise until flat wheel is just off ground.
3. **Swap Wheel**: Remove nuts fully, take off flat wheel, fit spare wheel.
4. **Tighten**: Put nuts back by hand, then tighten with spanner in a **star pattern** (opposite nuts one by one).
5. **Lower & Final Tighten**: Lower car fully, remove jack, and tighten all nuts firmly once more.
6. **Cleanup**: Put flat wheel and tools back in boot.

## Warnings
- **Do NOT** brake suddenly or jerk steering after a blowout.
- **Do NOT** change wheel on slopes, soft mud, or near traffic.
- **Do NOT** drive fast (>80 km/h) or far on the spare wheel.
- **Do NOT** drive on a fully flat tyre.
