This is the new Continental GT 750 which is all set to take Royal Enfield’s popular cafe racer formula a step further. While it retains the familiar retro silhouette of the Continental GT 650 but the new bike does come with some distinctive changes. The main highlight here is the new fairing which does make the bike look a lot more tempting than before. The overall design stays true to the cafe racer theme, although the larger proportions should give it a stronger road presence.
