## Living In The Suit

The Traverse comes with several features aimed at making this easier.

The jacket has touring storage and adjustment options, while the pants get integrated suspenders. There's also boot compatibility, reflective detailing and a 360-degree zip that connects the jacket and pants.

The jacket-pant connection is particularly useful because it allows the two pieces to function as a complete touring system. It also means you're not relying on two completely separate garments to do their jobs.

The pockets and storage options are worth looking at in the same way. When you're touring, you want the things you need to be accessible without having to unpack your motorcycle every time you stop.

The suspenders help keep the pants in place, while the adjustment points let you fine-tune the fit. Reflective detailing adds another practical touch for riding in low-light conditions.
