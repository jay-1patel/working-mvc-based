## Alto K10 Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Small and nimble, rides great over good or bad roads
- Ample space for four adults

**Could be Better**

- No day/night IRVM
- Manual wind-up windows in the rear
- Manual adjust for the ORVMs
- Fixed headrests
- Storage space in the rear
- Engine could be more refined

**Expert's Conclusion**

Entry level but not one dimensional. Comfortable for four adults and still is an engaging driving experience. Misses out on some comfort and practical features but it's still a great entry point into life on four wheels.
