## Long-Distance Riding

With great power comes great responsibility, and the ease of touring. While most scooters are discredited for touring because they can’t keep at highway speeds for long, the Ntorq 150 does it effortlessly. While riding down to Mumbai, and back, never did it feel out of breath or out of place. It can stay at highway speeds for extended periods. In fact, riding on the common patch of the Mumbai-Pune expressway did not feel unnerving at all. Only the bumpy surface prevented me from riding even faster.

While the seat is spacious enough for one, I felt a slight discomfort set in after two hours of saddle time.I considered taking a breather but Google Maps said I was not too far from home. So I decided to push on. Had I been on a longer ride, I would have definitely taken a break.

And not taking that break proved helpful since it increased my tolerance level a bit. The next day I rode the same distance back to Pune, without any noticeable discomfort.
