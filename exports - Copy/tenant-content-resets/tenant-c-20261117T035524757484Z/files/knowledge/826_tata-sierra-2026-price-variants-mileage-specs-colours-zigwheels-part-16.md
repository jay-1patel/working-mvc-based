## Tata Sierra FAQs

The boot space in Tata Sierra is 622 L.

What is the ground clearance of Tata Sierra?

The ground clearance of Tata Sierra is 205 mm.

How much power does the Tata Sierra make?

The Tata Sierra makes 105bhp@6000rpm in its Petrol form, measured on the variant the price starts from. It puts out 145Nm@2100rpm of torque.

What is the on-road price of Tata Sierra in Bhavnagar?

The Tata Sierra starts at ₹13.06 Lakh on-road in Bhavnagar for the base variant. On-road price includes RTO charges and insurance, so it is higher than the ex-showroom price.

Is Kia Seltos better than Tata Sierra?

The Tata Sierra starts at ₹11.49 Lakh against the Kia Seltos's ₹11.25 Lakh (ex-showroom). On-road in Bhavnagar it starts at ₹13.06 Lakh. It comes with driver, passenger and side front airbags.
