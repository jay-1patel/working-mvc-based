# Eco driving & car care (keep the car efficient)

Share these awareness tips when owners ask how to use or take care of the car so it stays fuel-efficient, cleaner and longer-lasting:

1. Warm up gently — avoid high RPM for the first 1–2 km after a cold start.
2. Smooth throttle and early upshifts save fuel; hard acceleration and late braking waste it.
3. Keep tyre pressure at the door-sticker PSI — under-inflated tyres cut mileage and raise wear.
4. Remove unused roof racks and heavy boot clutter; extra weight and drag reduce economy.
5. Use AC wisely — recirculate after cabin cools; park in shade when possible.
6. Follow the service schedule on time — clean oil, filters and plugs keep combustion efficient.
7. Prefer the recommended grade of engine oil and genuine filters for best economy and emissions.
8. Idle less than 30–60 seconds when waiting; switch off if safe and legal for longer stops.
9. Plan routes to avoid peak congestion when you can; steady 40–60 km/h city speeds are kinder on fuel.
10. Check PUC, battery terminals and coolant level monthly; fix warning lights promptly.
11. Wash with pH-neutral shampoo; avoid harsh chemicals on paint and plastic trims.
12. For long trips, check tyre pressure cold, top up washer fluid, and carry a basic tool/kit.
