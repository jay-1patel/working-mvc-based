# Eco Driving & Car Care

Keep your car efficient, cleaner, and longer-lasting with these tips:

1. **Warm Up Gently**: Avoid high RPM for the first 1–2 km after a cold start.
2. **Smooth Throttle**: Early upshifts save fuel; hard acceleration and late braking waste it.
3. **Tyre Pressure**: Keep at door-sticker PSI. Under-inflated tyres cut mileage and raise wear.
4. **Reduce Weight**: Remove unused roof racks and heavy boot clutter; extra weight and drag reduce economy.
5. **AC Wisely**: Recirculate after cabin cools; park in shade when possible.
6. **Follow Schedule**: Clean oil, filters, and plugs keep combustion efficient.
7. **Genuine Parts**: Prefer recommended grade of engine oil and genuine filters.
8. **Idle Less**: Idle less than 30–60 seconds when waiting; switch off if safe and legal.
9. **Plan Routes**: Avoid peak congestion; steady 40–60 km/h city speeds are kinder on fuel.
10. **Monthly Checks**: Check PUC, battery terminals, and coolant level; fix warning lights promptly.
11. **Washing**: Wash with pH-neutral shampoo; avoid harsh chemicals on paint and plastic trims.
12. **Long Trips**: Check tyre pressure cold, top up washer fluid, and carry a basic tool/kit.
