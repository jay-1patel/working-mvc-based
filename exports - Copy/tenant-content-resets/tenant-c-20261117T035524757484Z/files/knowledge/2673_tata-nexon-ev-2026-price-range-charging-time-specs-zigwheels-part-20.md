## Nexon EV Key Highlights

**Range:**
The Tata Nexon EV can go upto 465 Km after a full charge.

**Charging Time:**
The Tata Nexon EV long range battery takes 15 hours to get charged via a regular charger. A DC fast charger can replenish its battery from 10-80% in 56 minutes.

**Variants:**
The Tata Nexon EV comes in three main variants called the Creative, Fearless and Empowered. They further branch out to different variants with additional features. It is now also available in a Red Dark edition.
It is available in seven colour options: Daytona Grey, Pristine White, Flame Red, Creative Ocean, Fearless Purple, Empowered Oxide and Onyx Black.
