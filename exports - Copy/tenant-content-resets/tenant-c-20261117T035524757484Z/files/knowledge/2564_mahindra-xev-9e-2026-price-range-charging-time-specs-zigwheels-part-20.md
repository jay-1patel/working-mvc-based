## XEV 9e Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Two battery pack options & estimated real world range of 400-500km
- Superb infotainment experience: triple 12
- 3” screens + Harman/Kardon with Dolby Atmos
- Strong safety suite includes well-calibrated L2 ADAS & 5 Star BNCAP rated too

**Could be Better**

- High floorboard means you will have to watch your head when stepping in

**Expert's Conclusion**

The quality and execution of both the cabin experience and feature package could easily put to shame some luxury marques even up to three times this price point
