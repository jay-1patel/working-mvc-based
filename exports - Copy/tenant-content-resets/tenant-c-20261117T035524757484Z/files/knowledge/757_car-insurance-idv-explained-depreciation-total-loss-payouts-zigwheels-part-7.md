Yes, and usually by far more than you saved. Dialling down the IDV at renewal feels like a clever little hack because the premium quote drops instantly. The bill for that hack only arrives when the car is stolen or totalled.

It out with a three-year-old Maruti Suzuki Swift VXi bought for Rs 7 lakh, whose IDV after 30percent depreciation comes to Rs 4.9 lakh:

| **IDV chosen** | **Indicative premium** | **Maximum payout in total loss or theft** |
| Rs 4,00,000 (lowered to save premium) | Rs 8,500 | Rs 4,00,000 |
| Rs 4,90,000 (in line with market value) | Rs 10,500 | Rs 4,90,000 |
| Rs 5,50,000 (set higher) | Rs 12,000 | Rs 5,50,000 |
