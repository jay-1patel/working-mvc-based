## Overview

**Windsor Pro - A 'Pro' Move**

- Gets a larger 52.9 kWh battery (up from 40.55 kWh in the standard Windsor).
- MIDC-claimed range goes up to 449 km (an increase of 83 km).
- Real-world range is expected to be around 350-360 km, which is nearly 100 km more than the regular version.
- Level 2 ADAS introduced for the first time
- Powered tailgate added for extra convenience
- New 16-inch dual-tone alloy wheels
- New dual-tone ivory-and-black cabin (replaces the previous all-black interior)
- Three new exterior colours: Celadon Blue, Aurora Silver, and Glaze Red
- The bigger battery and premium features are only limited to the top-of-the-line 'Pro' variant.
