## About Renault

Renault India is known for its competitive pricing, offering budget-friendly cars that cater to the masses. Currently, it has three cars in its line-up: Kwid, Triber and Kiger.

Hatchback: Launched back in 2015, the Kwid took the market by a storm. It was the first SUV-styled entry-level hatchback in the Indian market at the time. But with changing consumer preferences, the entry-level hatchback segment seems to be losing its sheen and despite numerous additions and variations, the Kwid’s dominance has seen a downfall.

Sub-compact SUV: The Kiger is Renault’s offering in the crowded sub-compact SUV segment comprising the Nissan Magnite, Kia Sonet, and even the Tata Punch micro SUV among others.

People mover: Renault’s sub-4m crossover MPV, the Triber, was launched in 2019. With modular seating options, the MPV is best suited for those wanting a versatile and budget-friendly seven-seater.
