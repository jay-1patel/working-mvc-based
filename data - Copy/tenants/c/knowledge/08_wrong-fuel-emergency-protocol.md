# Wrong Fuel Filled

## Scenarios
- Petrol in Diesel car.
- Diesel in Petrol car.

## Symptoms
- Rough running, knocking, power loss, stalling, smoke (if engine started).

## Action Plan
### If Engine NOT Started:
1. Do NOT start the engine.
2. Push car to safe spot in Neutral.
3. Apply handbrake.

### If Engine STARTED:
1. Stop safely as soon as possible.
2. Switch engine OFF.
3. Note litres of wrong fuel.

## Critical Don'ts
- Do NOT start engine 'just to check'.
- Do NOT attempt DIY fuel tank drainage.
