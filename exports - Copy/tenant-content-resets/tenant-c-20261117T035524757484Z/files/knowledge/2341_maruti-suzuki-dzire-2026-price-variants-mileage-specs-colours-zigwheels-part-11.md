## Interiors

**What’s The Maruti Dzire Like Inside?**

- Design is similar to the Swift and just works
- Beige-black cabin theme looks upmarket and makes the cabin feel airier than the all-black Swift
- Super practical cabin has ample storage spaces
- Plastics on the dashboard are acceptable, but bits such as power window switches, light/wiper stalks feel budget-grade
- Fabric upholstery quality is strictly average
- Optional leatherette upholstery should have been offered in the top spec ZXI variant
- Misses a front central armrest
- Seats could have firmer cushioning for long journey comfort
