## About Tata

Tata Safari: First made-in-India 4WD SUV, popular for its bold looks and off-road capabilities.

Tata Nano: At the time of launch, the Tata Nano was priced at Rs 1 lakh, making it the most affordable production car in the world.

Tata Nexon EV: Most affordable and practical long-range electric vehicle in its space.

Tata Indigo: A value for money proposition, Tata Indigo was one of the highest selling cars for Tata.

Tata Indica: India's first fully indigenous vehicle, it ticked all the boxes for a hatchback at the time. It was also the most affordable diesel powered vehicle when it was launched in 1998.

Tata Sumo: A rugged utility vehicle, Tata Sumo was the go-to car capable of taking on rural territories with ease.

Tata Punch: The brand's smallest and most affordable SUV, the Tata Punch has been the best selling micro SUV since its arrival in 2021.
