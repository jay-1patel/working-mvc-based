Royal Enfield has collaborated with Hedon, a British premium helmet maker, to launch a handcrafted limited-edition helmet collection.

9 Oct, 2026

Deepanshi Chaudhary

The collection has two helmets: the Hedonist, a half-face, and the Heroine Racer 2.0 full-face.

These helmets are limited to only 16 units worldwide, each handcrafted to order and numbered.

These helmets are created to celebrate 125 years of Royal Enfield.

The half-face hedonist helmet costs GBP 1,650 (approx. Rs 2.11 lakh) and comes in six sizes, XS to XXL, weighing between 850 and 1,000 grams

The full-face helmet costs GBP 2,100 (approx. Rs 2.68 lakh) and comes in six sizes, S to XXXL, weighing between 1,200 and 1,390 grams

Both the helmets are ECE22.06 and DOT certified and come with a 1-year standard warranty.

Each helmet is made out of a composite shell of fibreglass and carbon fibre.
