Hero MotoCorp has launched the E85-compliant Splendor Flex Fuel and HF Deluxe Flex Fuel in India
4 Jun, 2026
Amey Deolalikar
Both motorcycles can run on fuel containing up to 85 percent ethanol (E85)
The Splendor Flex Fuel is priced at Rs 82,810, while the HF Deluxe Flex Fuel costs Rs 72,792 (ex-showroom, Delhi)
The Splendor Flex Fuel commands a premium of Rs 2,479 over the regular top-end Splendor Plus
The HF Deluxe Flex Fuel is just Rs 172 more expensive than the regular top-end HF Deluxe
Both bikes feature new graphics and exclusive colour schemes to distinguish them from standard models
The Splendor Flex Fuel’s 97.2cc engine produces 8.57PS and 8.3Nm, making it slightly more powerful than the regular version
The HF Deluxe Flex Fuel uses the same ethanol-compatible engine and delivers similar power and torque figures
Suspension, chassis, and braking hardware remain unchanged from the standard commuter motorcycles
