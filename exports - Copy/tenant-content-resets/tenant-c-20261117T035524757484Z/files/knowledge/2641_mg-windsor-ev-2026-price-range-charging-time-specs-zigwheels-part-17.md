## Windsor EV Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Unique looks, great build quality
- Impressive list of features and plush spacious interiors
- Unique warranty, buy-back and free charging options for new customers

**Could be Better**

- Compulsory 1500km billing per month under the BAAS scheme may not suit all
- Rear seat recline is comfortable but does eat into boot space
- Bigger battery-pack only available in the top-end variant

**Expert's Conclusion**

The Windsor promises a unique experience in the segment in terms of it's looks, comfortable & spacious interiors and purchasing experience. With a real-world range of around 250-260km, it is a great city runabout. Now, with the Pro variant in picture, it does offer a decent real-world range of 350-360 km, but unfortunately, it is only limited to the top-end variant.
