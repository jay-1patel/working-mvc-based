Say “Mahindra SUV” anywhere in India, and it brings to mind a name the nation trusts. For generations, families across the country have relied on Mahindra’s SUVs to take them wherever life leads, from mountain passes in the north to coastal highways in the south. Along the way, these SUVs have handled hill roads, village tracks and long highway journeys without missing a beat.

The Scorpio has been a big part of that story, and the Scorpio-N carries it forward. Its broad, upright body sits on a newly engineered ladder frame, and it gets two engines with real muscle. The 4x4 system has a proper low-range gearbox, and the suspension hardware is designed to make broken roads feel smoother. Put simply, it’s built the way a Mahindra SUV should be.
