## About Renault

**History:**

Founded by Louis Renault and his brothers, Renault is a French automaker established in 1898.

Renault came to India in 2005, but it wasn’t until 2007 that it launched its first car in the country, the Renault Logan. In 2010, the Renault-Nissan alliance set up a manufacturing facility in Chennai, which was completed in a record 21 months, and is capable of producing 480,000 vehicles annually. Renault has more than 525 sales and 530 service touchpoints in India, including 250+ Workshop On Wheels or mobile workshops across the country.

The French marque also has a prestigious motorsport heritage - not only is it one of the handful brands to compete in Formula 1, it’s also one of the most decorated ones; winning two constructors' and drivers' championships, while as an engine manufacturer it has 12 constructors' and 11 drivers' championships.
