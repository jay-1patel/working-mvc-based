Harley-Davidson has unveiled an all-new entry-level cruiser bike called the Sprint 440 at EICMA 2026. Developed in partnership with Hero MotoCorp, the new motorcycle aims to make the Harley-Davidson experience more accessible to a wider audience. Unlike the larger and more imposing motorcycles that the brand is known for, the Sprint 440 gets a lightweight, narrow profile and a relatively low seat height, making it more approachable for everyday riding. Powering the Sprint 440 is a 440cc, single-cylinder engine that produces 27 PS and 38 Nm. It is the same unit which also powers the X440 and is a long-stroke motor has been tuned to deliver strong low-end torque, making it suitable for city riding and relaxed highway cruising. It gets a six-speed gearbox and an assist-and-slipper clutch. The motorcycle also gets 43mm inverted fork, twin shock absorbers with preload adjustment and disc brakes at both ends with ABS.
