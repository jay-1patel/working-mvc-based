## About Renault

In February 2022, Renault announced that it has sold over 8 lakh cars in India - a milestone that can be attributed to three of its most popular models mentioned below:

Renault Duster: A rugged, value packed offering with a high ground clearance, powerful engine options and good ride quality. Sadly, Renault has discontinued the Duster after almost 10 years since it was first introduced.

Renault Kwid: A budget friendly, feature-loaded hatchback that is perfectly suited for the Indian roads. No wonder, the Kwid makes up for 50% of Renault’s sales in India, having sold over 4 lakh units!

Renault Triber: A sub-compact seven-seater offering with very aggressive pricing and decent set of features. Renault has sold over 1 lakh units of the Triber in India and to celebrate its success, also launched a limited-edition Triber LE in February 2022.
