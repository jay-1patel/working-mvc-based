## About KTM

Currently, the manufacturer is working on the RC 390 based on the 390 Duke’s platform and it has been spied testing already. It is expected to be unveiled at EICMA 2024. It will be powered by the same engine as that of the 390 Duke. Also, the next-gen 390 Adventure is also on the works and it has also been spied on multiple times now. It is expected to be unveiled by the end of 2024 as well.

**Product portfolio**

Nakeds - The Dukes have been the go-to sport machines for enthusiasts for over half a decade in India. Right from the smallest 125 Duke to the manic 390 Duke, each one of these nakeds adheres to KTM’s ‘Ready To Race’ mantra well. The 200 Duke has been a darling of Indian riders and if you are looking for a little sensibility from your Duke, the 250 Duke has you covered.
