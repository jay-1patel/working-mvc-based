## About Kia

**Product Portfolio**

SUVs: The Seltos, Syros, and sub-4m Sonet form Kia's SUV front. The Seltos rivals 5-seater SUVs such as the Hyundai Creta, Skoda Kushaq and VW Taigun. For the Sonet, India's sub-4m SUV segment is teeming with rivals such as the Tata Nexon, Maruti Suzuki Brezza, and Mahindra XUV 3XO.

People carriers: The Carnival was Kia's first people mover in India. It can seat anywhere from 7 to 9 people, and it slots above the Toyota Innova in the market. In late 2021 Kia introduced a 'recreational vehicle' (RV), the Carens three-row people carrier, followed by the Carens Clavis in 2025.

**Greatest Hits**

Kia's top products for India have been:

Kia Seltos: Tech loaded SUV in its segment

Kia Sonet: Better equipped for the price than many of its rivals and even larger SUVs

Kia Syros: Feature-rich subcompact SUV at an accessible price point
