Here we have Norton’s upcoming motorcycle which actually looks very different from what the brand has been known for. This is the Norton 450 Urban, which is actually not the official name of the bike, but it will be unveiled soon. The 450 cc roadster carries Norton’s new design language, and has given the bike a slightly modern, futuristic look. Powering the bike is a 450 cc single-cylinder, liquid-cooled motor. The brand has not yet unveiled its power figures, however it produces 43 Nm of torque. The bike is loaded with modern technology as well, it gets a large 8-inch touchscreen TFT console which is going to be a segment first. Along with this, the bike gets premium components, like inverted fork, monoshock, a big front disc with a four piston caliper, and radial tyres. Norton says that the bike will be launched next year, and will provide a more affordable entry-point into the Norton Motorcycles experience.
