## About Mahindra

Mahindra Thar: Initiated the Indian market to lifestyle SUVs with a relatively affordable civilian jeep

Mahindra XUV700: First Indian car to feature advanced driver assistance and active safety technologies

Bonus: Mahindra e2O: first EV from a mainstream Indian carmaker

**Future Plans:**

Currently, given the nascent market for EVs in India, Mahindra only offers the eVerito electric sedan. But the lineup is set to grow with the arrival of the eKUV100 and XUV300 mass-market electric SUVs. In addition, Mahindra plans to introduce the next-generation Scorpio SUV, 5-door Thar and multiple other SUVs in the near future.
