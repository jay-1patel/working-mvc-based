That gap is exactly why the Return to Invoice (RTI) add-on exists. According to RTI, it pays up to the car's invoice value, plus first-time registration charges and road tax, if the car is stolen or written off. Here's how the two stack up:

| **What you get in a total loss** | **Standard policy** | **Policy with Return to Invoice add-on** |
| Basis of payout | Insured Declared Value (IDV) | Original invoice value of the car |
| Road tax and registration | Not covered | Reimbursed |
| Impact of age-based depreciation | Fully reflected in payout | Gap between IDV and invoice is bridged |
| Best suited to | Older cars | New cars, typically in their first few years |

Bottom line: the IDV you click 'confirm' on at renewal is the IDV you'll be paid against on the worst day of your car's life. Choose like it matters, because it does.
