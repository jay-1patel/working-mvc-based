# MetroDrive Service Schedule Guide

## How to Calculate Due Date
Calculate months since purchase date and compare with odometer.

**Example:**
- Purchased: 15 Mar 2025
- Today: 15 Nov 2025 (~8 months)
- Odometer: 12,000 km
- **Result:** Due for 1st periodic service (10,000 km / 6 months already passed).

*Note: If only purchase date is known, use the time column and remind the customer to share km at booking.*

## Service Intervals
| Interval | Type | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **~10,000 km / 6 Months** | Ongoing Periodic | Repeat oil service; replace filters and wear parts when due. |
| **~24 Months / ~40,000 km** | 4th Periodic / Major | Oil service + spark plugs / coolant / brake fluid. |
| **Every 6 Months / ~10,000 km** | Ongoing | Repeat previous periodic services. |
| **Any Time** | Repair / Diagnosis | Scan, estimate, denting-painting or mechanical repair as required. |
