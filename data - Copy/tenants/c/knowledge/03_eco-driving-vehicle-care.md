# Eco Driving & Vehicle Care

Share these practical ownership tips when drivers ask how to preserve fuel economy, hybrid battery efficiency, and long-term reliability:

* Allow 30–45 seconds of gentle warm-up after a cold start and avoid high RPM during the first 2 km.
* Maintain progressive throttle inputs and anticipate traffic stops to maximize coasting and regenerative braking.
* Check cold tyre pressures every two weeks against the driver-door placard; under-inflation increases rolling resistance.
* Unload unused roof carriers and heavy boot cargo to reduce aerodynamic drag and kerb weight.
* Set cabin climate control to Auto / Recirculate once cooled and park in shaded bays whenever possible.
* Adhere strictly to 6-month / 10,000 km oil and filter intervals using manufacturer-specified viscosity grades.
* Switch off the engine at traffic halts exceeding 45 seconds where safe and permitted.
* Inspect coolant levels, battery terminals, and PUC validity monthly; address dashboard warning lamps immediately.
* Wash exterior paintwork with pH-neutral automotive shampoo and microfiber mitts to protect clear-coat and ceramic layers.
