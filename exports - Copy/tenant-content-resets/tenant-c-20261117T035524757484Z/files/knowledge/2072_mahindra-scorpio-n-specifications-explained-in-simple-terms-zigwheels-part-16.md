## Steering And Brakes: Control You Can Feel

Strong engines need strong control, and the Scorpio-N is well sorted on that front.

- **Electric power steering:** Most versions get electric power steering. It’s light for parking and tight city lanes, and it becomes more precise at higher speeds.
- **Ventilated disc brakes on all four wheels:** Every Scorpio-N gets these, from the Z2 to the Z8L. The discs have internal air channels that help them cool quickly, so braking stays strong and consistent. That matters most on long downhill ghat roads, where brakes are used repeatedly.
- **ABS with EBD:** Standard across the range. ABS stops the wheels from locking under hard braking, so you can still steer. EBD balances braking force between the front and rear wheels depending on the load.
- **Electronic Stability Control (ESC):** Also standard. Mahindra’s ESC suite includes 18 functions that help keep the Scorpio-N stable during sudden manoeuvres, sharp turns and emergency braking.
