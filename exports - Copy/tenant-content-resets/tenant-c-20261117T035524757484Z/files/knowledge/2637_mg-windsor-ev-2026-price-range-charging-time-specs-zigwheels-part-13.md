## Performance & Range

**A Decent Performer**

- Gets a 38 kWh battery mated to a FWD motor that produces 136 PS and 200 Nm.
- Claimed range of 331km with real-world expected range closer to 260-280 km.
- Four driving modes: Eco Plus, Eco, Normal, and Sport.
- Sport mode offers quicker acceleration, but is not thrilling in any manner.
- The 'Pro' variant with the bigger battery pack, offers a real-world range of about 350-360 km, which is about 100 km more than the regualr Windsor EV
- Regenerative braking adjustable through touchscreen which can be cumbersome due to the lack of any physical control.
- Efficient and predictable throttle response for city and highway driving, but lacks excitement.
