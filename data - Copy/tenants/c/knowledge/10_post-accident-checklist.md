# After an Accident: Step-by-Step Guide

## Immediate Actions
1. **Switch ON hazard lights.**
2. **Check for injuries** (self and passengers) first.
   - If anyone hurt: Call **112** immediately.
   - Do not move seriously injured person unless danger (fire/traffic).
3. **Move car** if safe and it can move. Otherwise, keep hazards on and place warning triangle behind car.
4. **Take everyone to a safe place** away from road.
5. **Exchange details** with other driver: Names, phone numbers, vehicle numbers. Note time and place.
6. **Take clear photos:** Damage to both cars, number plates, position on road, skid marks, traffic signals.
7. **Inform police** if injury, major damage, or dispute. File report for insurance.
8. **Inform insurance company** as early as possible.
9. **Call MetroDrive (+91 98765 43211):** Arrange tow if needed, help with cashless insurance claim.

## Do NOT Do This
- Leave spot without exchanging details.
- Argue or fight at the spot.
- Accept quick roadside repair for safety damage (steering, brakes, suspension, airbags).
- Sign blank paper or claim forms from unknown persons.
