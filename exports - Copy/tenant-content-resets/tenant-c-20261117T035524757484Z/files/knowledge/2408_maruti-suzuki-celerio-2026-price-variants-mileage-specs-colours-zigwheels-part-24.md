## Best Hatchback Cars

The celerio is a good and sleek car.I found no problem in it's working and running.However, Last year the tcm broke down.I had to a cost of rs 78000/- to get it repaired.

One of the best entry level hatch back car.Value for money, Easy on pocket, 20+ mileage on petrol, Minimal vibrations (evident but it gives thrill) good resale value, Fun to drive.What else one could expect under 6 lakhs.After sale service is satisfactory as service centres may charge you unnecessary sometimes.
