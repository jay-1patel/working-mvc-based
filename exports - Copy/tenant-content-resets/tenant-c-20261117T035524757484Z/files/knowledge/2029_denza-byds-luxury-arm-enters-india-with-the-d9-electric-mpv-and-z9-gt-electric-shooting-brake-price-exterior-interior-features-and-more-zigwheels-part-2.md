## QUICK Highlights

- Denza, the luxury sub-brand of BYD, enters India with 2 new EV models
- D9 MPV takes on the likes of the Toyota Vellfire
- The Z9 GT takes a unique approach to sporty 5-door luxury EVs

Denza, the luxury arm of BYD, has set foot in India with two models, the D9 MPV and the Z9 GT. Both come as completely built unit (CBU) imports, with the D9 priced at Rs 90 lakh and the Z9 GT at Rs 1.56 Crore (ex-showroom). The D9 is for those who want a large, three-row electric MPV, while the Z9 GT is a, performance-focused 5-door EV that is the only shooting brake-type car on sale in India.
