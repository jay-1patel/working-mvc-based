## Wagon R Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Comfortable ingress and egress
- Spacious cabin
- Large boot
- Basic safety kit on all variants

**Could be Better**

- Weak cabin insulation
- Fixed rear headrests
- Zxi variant not fully loaded
- 1 Star Safety Rating for Adult and 0 star for Child in GNCAP

**Expert's Conclusion**

The WagonR continues to be a sensible practical hatchback. It’s now stronger, more feature loaded. Importantly, the prices have not gone up substantially.
