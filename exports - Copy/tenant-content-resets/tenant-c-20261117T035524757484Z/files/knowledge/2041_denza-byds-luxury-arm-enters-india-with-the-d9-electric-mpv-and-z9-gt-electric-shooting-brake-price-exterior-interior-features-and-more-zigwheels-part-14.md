## ZigSays...

Denza has picked two very different cars to introduce itself in India. Out of the D9 and Z9 GT, the former is the more practical of the two. It slots in as a plush, all-electric alternative to luxury MPVs like the Toyota Vellfire, and sits above the MG M9. The Z9 GT is the showpiece and the eye-candy. It is the only shooting brake on sale on our shores, with the BMW i7, Porsche Taycan and the Mercedes-Benz EQS as one of its closest rivals.

Both these cars will give BYD a distinct luxury offering in India that goes well beyond its existing lineup. Since Indians are moving towards luxury MPVs, there is a potential market for the D9 to capture. On the other hand, the Z9 could be a solid weekend car to have fun with, while also offering enough space inside to take your family for a joyride.
