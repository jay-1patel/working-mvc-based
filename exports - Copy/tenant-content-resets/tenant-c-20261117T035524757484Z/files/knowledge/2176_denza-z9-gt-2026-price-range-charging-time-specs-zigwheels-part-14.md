## Denza Z9 GT FAQs

What is the price of top model of Denza Z9 GT?

What color options are available for Denza Z9 GT?

The Denza Z9 GT  is available in the following color options: Rime Gold, Obsidian Black and Fjord Green. View colour images of Z9 GT.

What are the top competitors of Denza Z9 GT?

What is the boot space in Denza Z9 GT?

How much power does the Denza Z9 GT make?

The Denza Z9 GT makes 1139.87bhp, measured on the variant the price starts from. It puts out 1210Nm of torque.

What is the on-road price of Denza Z9 GT in Delhi?

The Denza Z9 GT starts at ₹1.64 Crore on-road in Delhi for the base variant. On-road price includes RTO charges and insurance, so it is higher than the ex-showroom price.
