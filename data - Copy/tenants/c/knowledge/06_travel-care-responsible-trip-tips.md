# Travel Care & Responsible Trip Tips

• Carry original photo ID for every traveller; names on tickets must match ID spelling.
• Pack light layers, sunscreen, a reusable water bottle and any regular medicines with prescriptions.
• For wildlife parks: stay inside the vehicle, no littering, no loud music, follow guide instructions.
• At temples and heritage sites: dress modestly, follow photography rules, remove footwear where required.
• Prefer refillable bottles over single-use plastic; dispose waste only in marked bins.
• Keep digital and paper copies of vouchers, tickets and emergency contacts offline on your phone.
• Inform us of mobility needs, child ages or medical conditions before finalising hotels and vehicles.
• Reach pickup points 10–15 minutes early; long-distance road timing depends on traffic and weather.
• Buy travel insurance for multi-city or international trips; understand deductible and claim steps.
• On international trips: check passport validity (usually 6+ months), visa sticker/e-visa and forex limits.
• Respect local communities — ask before photographing people; support local handicrafts fairly.
• If plans change mid-trip, message the on-trip number on your voucher before leaving the hotel.
