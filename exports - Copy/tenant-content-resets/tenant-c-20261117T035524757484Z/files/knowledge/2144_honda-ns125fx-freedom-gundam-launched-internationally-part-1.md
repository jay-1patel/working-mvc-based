Honda has launched the limited-edition NS125FX Freedom Gundam edition in China.
11 Oct, 2026
Deepanshi Chaudhary
This 125 cc scooter is based on the anime Mobile Suit Gundam SEED.
It was first showcased at Bilibili World 2026 in China, which is an anime and gaming expo.
It gets the white colour with red and blue accents and ZGMF-X10A Freedom Gundam graphics on side panels.
The wheels are painted red and blue, and the front of the seat gets the Gundam Seed logo and anime-inspired graphics all over the scooter.
It is powered by a 124 cc air-cooled engine making 9.5 PS at 7,750 rpm and 10 Nm at 5,750 rpm
The fuel tank capacity is 5.7 litres with a claimed mileage of 50 kmpl.
This scooter features a coloured LCD console, USB-A and USB-C charging ports and single-channel ABS.
The seat height is 740 mm, and the scooter weighs 106 kg kerb.
