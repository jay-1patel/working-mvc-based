# Eco Driving & Car Care

Share these awareness tips when owners ask how to use or take care of the car so it stays fuel-efficient, cleaner and longer-lasting:

*   Warm up gently — avoid high RPM for the first 1–2 km after a cold start.
*   Smooth throttle and early upshifts save fuel; hard acceleration and late braking waste it.
*   Keep tyre pressure at the door-sticker PSI — under-inflated tyres cut mileage and raise wear.
*   Remove unused roof racks and heavy boot clutter; extra weight and drag reduce economy.
*   Use AC wisely — recirculate after cabin cools; park in shade when possible.
*   Follow the service schedule on time — clean oil, filters and plugs keep combustion efficient.
*   Prefer the recommended grade of engine oil and genuine filters for best economy and emissions.
*   Idle less than 30–60 seconds when waiting; switch off if safe and legal for longer stops.
*   Plan routes to avoid peak congestion when you can; steady 40–60 km/h city speeds are kinder on fuel.
*   Check PUC, battery terminals and coolant level monthly; fix warning lights promptly.
*   Wash with pH-neutral shampoo; avoid harsh chemicals on paint and plastic trims.
*   For long trips, check tyre pressure cold, top up washer fluid, and carry a basic tool/kit.

If the customer wants a personal eco check (tyre pressure, filter due date, driving tips for their model), offer Book Service or a callback from the service advisor.
