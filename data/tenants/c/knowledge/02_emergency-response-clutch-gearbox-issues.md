# Emergency Response: Clutch & Gearbox Issues

## Symptoms
- Stuck or too loose clutch pedal.
- Burning smell after driving in heavy traffic.
- Car jerks or slips when moving.
- Gears will not engage.

## Immediate Actions (Do This Now)
1. **If the car will not move:** Stop trying. Switch ON hazard lights and park safely with the handbrake on.
2. **If the car moves but jerks:** Drive slowly only to the nearest safe parking place. Do not start a long journey.
3. **Inspect:** Look at the ground under the car for oil leaks and note the colour. Tell the service advisor this helps diagnosis.
4. **Cool Down:** If the problem started after heavy traffic or hill (ghat) driving, let the car cool for 20 to 30 minutes. An overheated clutch or gearbox sometimes works again when cool — but it still needs a workshop check.

## What NOT To Do
- Do NOT keep revving the engine and forcing the gear in. It damages the gearbox and clutch further.
- Do NOT rest your foot on the clutch pedal while driving.
- Do NOT hold the car standing on a slope using only the clutch. Both burn the clutch out quickly.

## Possible Causes
- Worn-out clutch.
- Low or old gearbox oil.
- Broken gear cable or linkage.
- Fault inside the gearbox.
- Burning smell after traffic usually means the clutch has been slipping.
