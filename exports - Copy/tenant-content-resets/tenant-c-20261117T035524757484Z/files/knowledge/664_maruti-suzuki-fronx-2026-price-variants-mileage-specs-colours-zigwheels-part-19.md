## FRONX Key Highlights

**Variants:**
The Maruti Fronx is available in six broad trims: Sigma, Delta, Delta+, Delta+(O), Zeta and Alpha. The Fronx gets seven monotone colours: Celestial Blue, Arctic White, Splendid Silver, Grandeur Gray, Earthen Brown, Opulent Red and Bluish Black. You can also opt for dual-tone shades with either the Earthen Brown, Splendid Silver or Opulent Red paired with a Bluish Black Roof.
