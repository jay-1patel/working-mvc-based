Declared accessories and fitments - Make sure aftermarket additions such as a CNG kit, music system or alloy wheels are listed, or they will not count in a total-loss payout.

Your car's depreciation bracket - Check whether the car has just moved into a new age slab, since IDV and parts depreciation both step up at those points.

Return to Invoice eligibility - For a car in its early years, check whether RTI is available and worth adding to close the gap between IDV and the on-road price.

Zero depreciation and its claim limit - Confirm it is active and note how many claims per year it covers, since insurers point out that standard depreciation applies once that limit is used up.

Outstanding loan versus IDV - If the car is financed, make sure the IDV is not lower than what you still owe the lender.

EV-specific protection - For electric cars, confirm whether the battery, charger and charging cable are covered, and add an EV-focused add-on if they are not.
