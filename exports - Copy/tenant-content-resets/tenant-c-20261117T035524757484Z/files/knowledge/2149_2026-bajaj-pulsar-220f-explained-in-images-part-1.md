The Bajaj Pulsar 220F has received an update for 2026, finally getting an LED headlamp and a new colour option.

19 Jun, 2026

Amogh Wadikhaye

The bike looks a bit more modern now.

It still gets a semi-fairing, clip-on handlebars, and a split seat with split grab rails.

The bike is available in four colour options: Black Gold, Black Cherry Red, Black Ink Blue, and Black Copper Beige.

The new Black Gold colour option gets a black base paint with attractive gold graphics, along with carbon-fibre stickers, giving the bike a premium touch.

The Black Cherry Red colour gets a black base paint with bright red graphics. This colour is great for someone who likes attention.

The Black Ink Blue colour gets blue graphics on the tank and the semi-fairing, making it look sporty and youthful.

The Black Copper Beige colour gets copper graphics that look stealthy. It is perfect for someone who wants an understated look.
