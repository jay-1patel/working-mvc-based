## Alto K10 Key Highlights

**Variants:**
There are four trim options available: Std(O), LXi, VXi, and VXi+. The VXi trim is the only one to get the option of a CNG kit.
You can spec the hatchback in six colour hues: Solid White, Metallic Silky Silver, Metallic Granite Grey, Metallic Sizzling Red, Pearl Metallic Earth Gold and Metallic Speedy Blue.

**Safety Rating:**
The Alto K10 has scored a 2 star safety rating in NCAP crash tests.
