# Why Devotees Visit Divine Guide

*   **Darshan of the Divine Guide Lingam:** Believed to be swayambhu (self-manifested); aarti darshan here is considered especially fruitful on Mondays and in Shravan.
*   **Ganga snan at Har Ki Pauri:** The temple is a 5-minute walk from the ghat where the Ganga is worshipped every evening.
*   **Seva opportunities:** Rudrabhishek, Aarti Yajman, Annadan (feeding devotees) and Gau Seva at the temple gaushala can all be booked through the assistant or the seva desk.
*   **Peaceful mornings:** The 5:00 AM Mangala Aarti and the hour after it are the quietest time for meditation in the temple hall.

# Facilities that make the visit easy

*   Free wheelchair service and a ramp at Gate 1; seating inside the sanctum hall for senior citizens.
*   Free drinking water (RO) and shoe-keeping counters at both gates.
*   Annakshetra bhandara — a free, satvik midday meal for every devotee, daily 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM.
*   Clean washrooms and a first-aid desk near Gate 2; a doctor is on call through the office.
*   Special queue for senior citizens (above 65), pregnant women and devotees with disabilities — no pass needed, ask any sevak.
