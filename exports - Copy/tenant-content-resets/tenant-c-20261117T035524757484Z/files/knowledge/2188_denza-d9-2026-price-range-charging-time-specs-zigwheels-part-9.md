## Denza D9 FAQs

What is the price of top model of Denza D9?

The on-road price of  top variant of Denza D9 is ₹94.57 Lakh (Delhi).

What color options are available for Denza D9?

The Denza D9  is available in the following color options: Whale Sea Blue, Starry Night Grey, Cosmos Black and Arctic White. View colour images of D9.

What are the top competitors of Denza D9?

The top 3 competitors of Denza D9 are Tesla Roadster, MG Cyberster and Kia EV6.

What is the boot space in Denza D9?

The boot space in Denza D9 is 689 L.

How much power does the Denza D9 make?

The Denza D9 makes 455.95bhp, measured on the variant the price starts from. It puts out 365Nm of torque.

What is the on-road price of Denza D9 in Delhi?

The Denza D9 starts at ₹94.57 Lakh on-road in Delhi for the base variant. On-road price includes RTO charges and insurance, so it is higher than the ex-showroom price.

How much time does a Denza D9 take to fully charge?

D9 taking DC 10%-97%: 9 min to get fully charged.
