## Infotainment

**What Infotainment Features Does The XEV 9e Offer?**

- 12.3 inch digital instrument cluster with multi-views and real-time ADAS display
- 12.3 inch touchscreen infotainment system with wireless Android Auto/Apple CarPlay
- 16-speaker Harman Kardon music system that delivers excellent sound quality
- On-board selfie camera with photo and video modes
- The front passenger’s screen can be used to view content on YouTube or OTT apps, play games and it even links to the interior camera for video calls on Zoom or Google Meets
- Groove Me light and music show that engages the exterior lights, ambient lights, pop-out door handles and wing mirrors
- Digital instrument cluster response times could’ve been quicker
- The front passenger’s screen remains active even when the car is on the move which can be distracting for the driver
