## About Honda

Honda Cars made its debut in India in 1995 with models like the City and the Accord. The Honda City continues to be the strongest product in Honda’s India lineup followed by models like the Amaze and the Civic. In the SUV space, Honda offers the 7-seater CR-V as well as smaller SUVs like the Jazz-based WR-V and the BR-V. Discontinued Honda models in India include the Brio and Mobilio. While Honda used to sell petrol-only models in India for the first 15 years, the carmaker began selling diesel cars in 2013 with the first product being the Amaze. Honda is known for its engines and is often remarked as the best engine manufacturer in the world. Like other Japanese carmakers, Honda is also a name that stands for stellar reliability. The diesel engines go by the name i-DTEC while the petrol engines are called i-VTEC. If there’s one thing that’s missing from Honda India’s portfolio, that’ll be some fun-to-drive cars.
