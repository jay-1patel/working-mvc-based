## Safety

**What About Safety Features?**

- Features include ABS with EBD, ESP. 7 airbags, front and rear parking sensors, ISOFIX child seat mounts, all-wheel disc brakes
- Also gets 360-degree camera with indicator linked display in the instrument cluster
- 360-degree camera includes Secure360 which keeps monitoring on even when the car is switched off
- Advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) include auto-braking, adaptive cruise control, lane-assist, auto lane-switch and overtaking, rear crossing traffic alert and high-beam assist
- Auto park assist which can be operated from the key, phone app or while seated in the car.
- Supports parallel, perpendicular or angular parking possible
- Auto-park assist doesn’t account for changes in road depth and may not work correctly where road-level changes are involved
