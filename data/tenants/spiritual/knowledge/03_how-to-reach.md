# Directions & Travel Guidance

**Address:** Divine Guide Temple, Har Ki Pauri Road, Haridwar, Uttarakhand 249401. The temple is a 5-minute walk from Har Ki Pauri ghat.

### By Train
Haridwar Junction (HW) is 2 km away — about 10 minutes by auto or e-rickshaw (Rs. 50–80). Trains connect Haridwar to Delhi, Dehradun, Mumbai, Ahmedabad and most major cities.

### By Air
Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun — 38 km, about 1 hour 15 minutes by taxi (Rs. 1,200–1,500). Pre-paid taxis are available at the airport.

### By Bus
Haridwar ISBT bus stand is 1.5 km away. Regular buses from Delhi (ISBT Kashmere Gate, about 5–6 hours), Dehradun, Rishikesh and Chandigarh.

### Own Vehicle
Delhi to Haridwar is about 220 km via NH-334 (5–6 hours). Park at the temple parking (see Vehicle Type) — private vehicles are not allowed in the Har Ki Pauri lane.

### Local Travel
E-rickshaws and autos are available outside the station and bus stand; agree the fare before starting.

**Note:** The last 300 metres to the temple are a walking lane — elderly devotees can use the free wheelchair service at Gate 1. During Kumbh, Ardh Kumbh and Kanwar Yatra, routes and parking change on government orders — ask the assistant or the office for the current arrangement before travelling.
