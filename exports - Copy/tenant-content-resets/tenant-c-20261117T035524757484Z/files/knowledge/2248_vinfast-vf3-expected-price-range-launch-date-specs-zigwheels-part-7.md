## Questions & Answers on VinFast VF3

Q. Does the VF3 support fast charging?
-
With a fast charger, the battery can be charged from 10 to 70 percent in just 36 minutes.

Q. How many driving modes are available in the VinFast VF3?
-
The VinFast VF3 electric vehicle (EV) has three driving modes: Eco, Comfort, and Sport.

Q. What are the key safety features of the VinFast VF3?
-
The VinFast VF3 offers key safety features including multiple airbags, advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), ABS with EBD, electronic stability control (ESC), and a rearview camera. These features ensure enhanced safety for both the driver and passengers.
