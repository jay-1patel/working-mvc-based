This time the action is split across two venues. The main event, the Auto Expo Motor Show, takes over Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan, from February 4 to 9, while Yashobhoomi in Dwarka runs from February 4 to 7.

If you care more about components, construction machines or flying taxis than the latest SUV, head to Dwarka before it wraps up early.

Entry was free in 2025, but this time you’ll need a ticket. It still costs less than a decent multiplex outing:

February 4: Media and industry only

Weekdays: Rs 300 per person, 10 AM to 7 PM

Weekends: Rs 200 per person, 10 AM to 6 PM

Visitor registration will open on the official Bharat Mobility Global Expo website, so keep an eye on it.

The Bharat Mobility Global Expo is more than a motor show. Bharat Mandapam will host the Auto Expo Motor Show, the India International Tyre Show, the Bharat Battery Show, the India Cycle Show, a Mobility Tech Pavilion and a Steel Pavilion.
