# Eco Driving & Car Care

Keep your car efficient, cleaner, and longer-lasting with these tips:

## Driving Habits
- **Warm up gently:** Avoid high RPM for the first 1–2 km after a cold start.
- **Smooth driving:** Smooth throttle and early upshifts save fuel; hard acceleration and late braking waste it.
- **Tyre Pressure:** Keep tyres at the door-sticker PSI. Under-inflated tyres cut mileage and raise wear.
- **Weight & Drag:** Remove unused roof racks and heavy boot clutter.
- **AC Usage:** Use AC wisely—recirculate after cabin cools; park in shade when possible.
- **Idling:** Idle less than 30–60 seconds when waiting; switch off if safe and legal for longer stops.
- **Route Planning:** Avoid peak congestion; steady 40–60 km/h city speeds are kinder on fuel.

## Maintenance
- **Service Schedule:** Follow it on time. Clean oil, filters, and plugs keep combustion efficient.
- **Parts:** Prefer the recommended grade of engine oil and genuine filters.
- **Monthly Checks:** Check PUC, battery terminals, and coolant level. Fix warning lights promptly.

## Exterior Care**
- **Washing:** Use pH-neutral shampoo; avoid harsh chemicals on paint and plastic trims.

## Long Trips**
- Check tyre pressure when cold.
- Top up washer fluid.
- Carry a basic tool/kit.
