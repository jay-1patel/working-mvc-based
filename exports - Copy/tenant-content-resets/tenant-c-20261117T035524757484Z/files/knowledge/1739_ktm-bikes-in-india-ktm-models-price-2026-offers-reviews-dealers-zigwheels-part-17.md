## About KTM

**History**

KTM India has recently updated the RC lineup for 2024, with both the RC 390, RC 200 and RC 125 receiving new colour schemes and blacked-out front indicators. In 2023, KTM launched the spoked wheel variant of hte 390 Adventure with adjustable suspension called the KTM 390 Adventure SW. The spoked wheel variant is now the top-spec variant in the 390 Adventure lineup. After that, a few months later, KTM launched the all-new KTM 390 Duke and KTM 250 Duke in India. The new 390 Duke is an all-new bike with a new chassis, bigger engine, fully adjustable suspension and an extensive list of features. The KTM 250 Duke is also based on the same platform and gets a revised chassis along with an all-new engine.
