## Maruti Suzuki FRONX On Road Price in Bhavnagar - Starts at ₹8.03 Lakh

Updated on November 2026

The Maruti Suzuki FRONX on-road price in Bhavnagar starts at ₹8,02,576 for the base Sigma variant, which includes an ex-showroom price of ₹6,89,853, RTO charges of ₹75,291, and insurance of ₹37,432. The top-spec Alpha Turbo DT AT variant is priced at ₹13,76,425 on-road. Maruti Suzuki offers 14 FRONX variants across petrol and CNG, with manual and automatic transmission options.
