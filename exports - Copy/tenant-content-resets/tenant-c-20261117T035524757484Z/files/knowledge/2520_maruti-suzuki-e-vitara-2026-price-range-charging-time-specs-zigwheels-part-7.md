## e Vitara Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Looks handsome & proportionate and still distinctive from its ICE stablemate the Grand Vitara, Two battery packs and over 500 km of range, Feature rich cabin and full safety suite including 7-airbags and level 2 ADAS - a first for a Maruti

**Could be Better**

- No AWD variant makes us sad

**Expert's Conclusion**

Promises to be an all-rounded exciting EV offering in one of the most competitive segments.
