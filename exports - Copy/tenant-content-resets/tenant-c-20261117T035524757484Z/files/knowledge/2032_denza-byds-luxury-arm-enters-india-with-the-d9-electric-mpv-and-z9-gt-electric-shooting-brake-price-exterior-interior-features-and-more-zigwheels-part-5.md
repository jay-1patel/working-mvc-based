## Exterior

The D9 boasts an upright face dominated by a large, blanked-off grille with vertical patterned elements. The grille is flanked by thick silver strips that merge into the LED DRLs, which sit below the dual-pod LED projector headlamps. The lower bumper gets a separate set of LED DRLs. The tall and flat-ish fascia is typical of a luxury MPV.

In profile, the D9 measures over 5.2 metres in length with a wheelbase of around 3.1 metres. It has a straight beltline and roofline, 18-inch dual-tone alloy wheels and powered sliding doors with a soft-close function.

The rear end features a chunky full-width LED tail-light bar, Denza lettering on the powered tailgate and a silver strip on the lower bumper that adds some contrast. It also gets a roof-mounted spoiler for a sporty look.
