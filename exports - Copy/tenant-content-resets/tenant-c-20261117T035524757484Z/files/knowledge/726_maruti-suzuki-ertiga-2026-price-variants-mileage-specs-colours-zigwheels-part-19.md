## Ertiga Key Highlights

**Variants:**
The Maruti Suzuki Ertiga is available in four broad trims: Lxi, Vxi, Zxi and Zxi+. Colour options include seven monotone shades: Auburn Red, Dignity Brown, Magma Grey, Oxford Blue, Arctic White, Splendid Silver and Bluish Black. The last shade is not available in the base Lxi variant.
