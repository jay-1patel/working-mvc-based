## About Honda

When choosing between the Honda Amaze and Honda City, it largely depends on individual preferences and requirements. The Amaze is a compact sedan ideal for city commuting, featuring a 1.2L petrol engine with 89 bhp and 110 Nm of torque, and a fuel efficiency of 18.65–19.46 km/l. It comes with advanced safety features like Honda Sensing, six airbags, and vehicle stability assist. The City, a mid-size sedan, offers a more premium experience with a 1.5L petrol engine producing 119 bhp and 145 Nm of torque, fuel efficiency of 17.8–18.4 km/l, a spacious cabin, advanced infotainment, and safety technologies including ADAS.
