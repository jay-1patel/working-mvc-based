## About Vespa

Currently, Vespa offers a bunch of scooters in India with two engine options - the ZX in 125cc, the SXL and Racing Sixties - with both 125cc and 150cc engine and the Elegante, which only comes with the 150cc engine. All the models that Vespa offers in India come with the retro chic design and bright colors that made it such a global icon. Some years ago, Piaggio announced that they would be making an electric Vespa specifically designed for India. But thanks to the multiple lockdowns and market slowdowns caused by the Covid-19 pandemic seems to have derailed those plans for now.
