# Prasad Offerings & Timings

| Prasad | Where | Timings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Charanamrit & mishri (free, after every aarti) | Main sanctum exit | After each aarti, while devotees are present |
| Morning prasad (khichdi, on festival days) | Annakshetra hall | 8:00 AM – 9:30 AM |
| Prasad boxes (Rs. 101 / Rs. 251) | Prasad counter, main exit | 7:00 AM – 8:30 PM |
| Bhandara meal (free for all) | Annakshetra hall | 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM daily |

**Good to know:**
*   Prasad offered by devotees (flowers, fruits, sweets) is accepted at the sanctum counter between 5:30 AM and 8:00 PM. Please do not bring prasad in plastic bags — paper or cloth bags are provided free at the counter.
*   Coconut is broken only at the designated place near Gate 1, not inside the sanctum.
*   Prasad by post can be ordered through the assistant or the seva desk (+91 98765 43211) — dry prasad only, Rs. 351 + courier, delivered in 4–7 days.
*   On Ekadashi, Purnima and Mahashivratri, special prasad (sabudana khichdi / fruit prasad) is distributed after the morning and evening aartis.
