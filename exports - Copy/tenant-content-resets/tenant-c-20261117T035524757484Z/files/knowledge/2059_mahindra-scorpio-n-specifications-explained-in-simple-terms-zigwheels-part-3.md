## The Foundation: A Frame Built From Scratch

Every tough SUV starts with what’s underneath, so that’s where we’ll begin. The Scorpio-N uses body-on-frame construction, built on Mahindra’s next-generation 3G frame. Mahindra says the frame was engineered from scratch rather than carried over, and it uses high-strength steel throughout.

**What it means:**

- **Body-on-frame** means the body sits on a separate steel chassis. It’s how trucks and serious off-roaders are built, with a stiff backbone that can take years of rough roads, heavy loads and trail abuse.
- **Monocoque**, used by most crossovers, combines the body and chassis into one shell. That works well in the city, but it isn’t designed for the same punishment.
- **High-strength steel** makes the structure stiffer and stronger without adding unnecessary bulk.
