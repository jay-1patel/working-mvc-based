# Car Loan Documentation

We partner with HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, and Bajaj Finance.

## Required Documents
*   PAN Card
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Salary Slips: Last 3 months (or ITR for self-employed)
*   Bank Statements: Last 6 months
*   Cancelled Cheque

## Process
*   **Down Payment:** Typically 10–20% depending on profile.
*   **Pre-approval:** Often completed within 24–48 hours.
*   **Assistance:** Our desk helps complete the forms.
