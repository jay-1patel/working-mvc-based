## About Skoda

Sedans: Skoda’s sedan lineup consists of the brand’s global stalwarts, the Octavia and Superb sedans. Segment sales for the carmaker are primarily driven by the Slavia which is based on the MQB-A0-IN platform.

SUVs: The carmaker offers the Kushaq compact SUV at the entry level. The facelifted Kodiaq SUV is positioned towards the more premium side of the spectrum as it fends off competition from the likes of the MG Gloster and Jeep Meridian.

Greatest hits

2004 Skoda Octavia RS: Iconic performance sedan that’s still revered by the automotive community

Skoda Superb: Plethora of features, plentitude of rear seat space and a plush ride made it a proper European luxury sedan

2020 Skoda Octavia RS 245: Quickest Skoda in India with a massive tuning potential

Skoda Kushaq: First mass-market Skoda SUV that debuted the globally-derived MQB A0 IN platform

Skoda Rapid: Compact, highway-friendly family sedan with a solid build quality
