## About Hero

**Hero Impulse -** India’s first dual sport motorcycle. Although not a runaway sales success, garnered a massive cult following.

**Hero Xpulse 200 -** Hero’s follow-up to the Impulse - larger engine, more capability and an optional rally kit make it perfect for off-road enthusiasts.

**Hero Xtreme 250R** - Hero's step-up performance motorcycle above the Xtreme 160R, built around a 250cc engine. It signaled Hero's return to genuine performance territory decades after the Karizma first defined that space for the brand.

**Hero Karizma XMR** - 2023 revival of the Karizma nameplate as a fully faired sport-tourer, bringing the legendary name back with modern liquid-cooled performance and full digital instrumentation.

**Hero Mavrick 440** - Hero's most premium motorcycle to date, sharing its 440cc platform with the Harley-Davidson X440 (developed in partnership with Harley-Davidson), aimed at riders wanting a modern roadster with genuine highway muscle.
