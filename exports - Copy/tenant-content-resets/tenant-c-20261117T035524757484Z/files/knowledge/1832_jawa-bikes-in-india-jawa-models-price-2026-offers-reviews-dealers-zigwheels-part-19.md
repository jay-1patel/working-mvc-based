## About Jawa

Very smooth riding bike good breaking system and the seats are very comfortable mileage not bad.

i own this jawa perak and whenever i take it for a spin everyone just stares at it and asks about this bike. Amazing looks! The only problem i had with this bike is that its body always has some problems with nutshells and khatar sounds, apart from this everything is perfect. Mileage is good. Happy with it.
