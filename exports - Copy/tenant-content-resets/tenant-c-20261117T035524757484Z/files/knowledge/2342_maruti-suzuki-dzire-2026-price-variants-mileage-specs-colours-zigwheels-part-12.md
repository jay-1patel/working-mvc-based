## Space & Practicality

**Is It Spacious?**

- Driver’s seat can be adjusted for height, enough room for even six footers
- Second row has ample kneeroom and foot room
- Huge 386-litre boot space is more than sufficient for a long tripSeats adjust for height and steering adjusts for tilt
- Gives a good driving position for most body sizes
- Rear seat headroom is less than ideal, especially for those above 5 '10
- Rear occupants’ forward visibility is limited by huge headrests and low roofline (due to sunroof)
- Boot unlock button doesn't work if the keys aren’t in your pocket
