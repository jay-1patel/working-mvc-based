## Interiors

**Minimalist But A Premium Cabin**

- Premium cabin with soft-touch materials on the doors and dashboard.
- An extensive usage of faux wood trims and rose gold accents exude a premium appeal.
- Minimalist layout with a 2-spoke steering wheel, a massive touchscreen, a touch-enabled AC control panel and a digital driver’s display.
- Some lightweight plastics used in the centre console and door handles reduce the upmarket feel.
- Touchscreen controls and minimal physical buttons can feel complicated to use. Can be tricky to explain if other members in the family use the same car.
