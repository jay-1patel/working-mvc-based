## Actual Comfort

Mobility and ventilation are important, but neither tells you the whole story.

The real question is how the suit feels when you're wearing it for an extended period. The weight of the gear, the fit around the shoulders and knees, the feel of the armour and how easily you can move around all contribute to the experience.

The Traverse is a substantial touring suit, so the expectation isn't that you'll forget you're wearing it. What matters is whether it allows you to get on with the ride without constantly adjusting the fit or feeling restricted.

The multiple adjustment points help tailor the fit, while the stretch panels allow for movement. The jacket and pants are also designed to work together rather than as two completely separate pieces of riding gear.
