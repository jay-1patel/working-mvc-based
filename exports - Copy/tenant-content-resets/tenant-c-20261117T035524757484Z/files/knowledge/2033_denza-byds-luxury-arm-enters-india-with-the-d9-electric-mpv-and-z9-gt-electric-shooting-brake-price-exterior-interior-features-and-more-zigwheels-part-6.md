## Interior

The D9 gets a seven-seat layout, featuring 2 seats at the front, two in the second row and three in the last row. The cabin gets Nappa leather upholstery, along with wood inserts and suede trims. To elevate the comfort, the second row gets individual captain seats with all the features that you would expect. Rear passengers have dedicated touchscreen controls in the armrests.

Similar to most BYD models, it gets a large 15.6-inch infotainment screen and plenty of storage spaces in the lower centre console. The flat bottom, two-spoke steering wheel further gives the interior a clean look.

The good thing is that all passengers get dedicated AC vents and grab handles.
