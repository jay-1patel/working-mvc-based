## Screens And Tech: The Numbers Inside

The Scorpio-N’s cabin tech has its own set of specs. Mahindra lists screen sizes in centimetres, so here they are converted to inches:

| **Feature** | **Size** | **Available on** |
| Touchscreen | 12.3 inches | Z8S and above |
| Digital driver’s display | 10.25 inches | Z8S and above |
| Touchscreen | 9 inches | Z2 |
| Touchscreen | 8 inches | Z4, Z6 |

The 12.3-inch screen runs on a Qualcomm 6155 chipset and Mahindra’s Adrenox software. Through a smartphone app, it gives you access to more than 70 connected car features, including remote engine start, remote climate control and geo-fencing. Other tech highlights include:

- **Alexa built-in:** Voice control for the infotainment system.
