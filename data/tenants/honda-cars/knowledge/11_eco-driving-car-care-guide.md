# Eco Driving & Car Care

Keep your car efficient, cleaner, and longer-lasting with these tips:

## Driving Habits
- **Warm Up Gently:** Avoid high RPM for the first 1–2 km after a cold start.
- **Smooth Throttle:** Early upshifts save fuel; hard acceleration and late braking waste it.
- **Tyre Pressure:** Keep at the door-sticker PSI. Under-inflated tyres cut mileage and raise wear.
- **Reduce Weight:** Remove unused roof racks and heavy boot clutter.
- **AC Usage:** Recirculate after cabin cools; park in shade when possible.
- **Idling:** Idle less than 30–60 seconds when waiting; switch off if safe/legal for longer stops.
- **Route Planning:** Avoid peak congestion; steady 40–60 km/h city speeds are kinder on fuel.

## Maintenance
- **Service Schedule:** Follow it on time. Clean oil, filters, and plugs keep combustion efficient.
- **Oil Grade:** Prefer the recommended grade of engine oil and genuine filters.
- **Monthly Checks:** Check PUC, battery terminals, and coolant level. Fix warning lights promptly.
- **Washing:** Use pH-neutral shampoo; avoid harsh chemicals on paint and plastic trims.
- **Long Trips:** Check tyre pressure cold, top up washer fluid, and carry a basic tool/kit.
