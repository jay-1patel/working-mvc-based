## What Is ISRL?

The Indian Supercross Racing League is a motorcycle racing championship where teams compete against each other with professional Indian and international riders. It is held on specially built dirt tracks with jumps, obstacles, and sharp turns.

It features four categories: 450cc International, 250cc International, 250cc India & Asia Mix, and 250cc Indian Futures. The new Indian Futures category was introduced this season to give Indian riders an opportunity to compete at an international level.
