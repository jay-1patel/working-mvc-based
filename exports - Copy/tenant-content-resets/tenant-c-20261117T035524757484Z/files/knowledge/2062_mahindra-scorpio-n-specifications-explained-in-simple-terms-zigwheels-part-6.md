## The Engines: Mahindra Muscle, Two Ways

- **Power (PS):** Power determines how quickly you build speed and how easily you pass a slow-moving truck on the highway. The petrol’s 203 PS stays the same on every variant that offers it, from the base Z2 to the top Z8L.
- **Torque (Nm):** Torque is the pulling force you feel when you press the accelerator. In an SUV this size, it decides whether the drive feels relaxed or strained, especially with seven people on board and a full boot. The diesel automatic’s 400 Nm makes even a fully loaded Scorpio-N feel easy to drive.
- **The rpm band:** This tells you when the torque is available. On the diesel, the pull starts from 1500 rpm, which is the range you use every day in the city. You get strong acceleration without revving the engine hard.
- **Turbocharged direct injection (TGDi):** The turbocharger forces more air into the engine, and direct injection sprays fuel straight into each cylinder.
