## Tata Nexon EV

Updated: November 2026. Tata Nexon EV on-road price in Bhavnagar starts at ₹14.40 Lakh and goes up to ₹20.39 Lakh. It offers 30 kWh and 45 kWh battery packs with a claimed range of 275–489 km (MIDC/ARAI) and DC fast charging. It rivals the Hyundai Bayon, Mahindra Thar OG, Mahindra Scorpio N and Maruti Suzuki Brezza.
