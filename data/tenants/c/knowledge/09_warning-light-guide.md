# Warning Light Guide

## Red Lights (STOP)
- **Brake Fault / Low Fluid:** Brakes may fail. Stop safely, do not drive, call for tow.
- **Battery / Charging:** Alternator fault. Car will stop when battery dies. Switch off AC/lights/music. Drive only short distance to safe place or call for help.

## Amber Lights (ATTEND SOON)
- **Check Engine:** Engine/emissions fault. Steady: Book check. Flashing: Slow down and stop soon.
- **ABS:** Anti-lock braking fault. Normal brakes work but wheels can lock. Drive carefully, extra distance, book check.
- **Airbag:** System fault. Airbags may not open. Book check earliest. Take extra care.
- **TPMS (Tyre Pressure):** Low air. Check tyres at next stop, fill to pressure. If flat, follow flat tyre procedure.
- **Fuel Low:** Fill fuel at nearest pump. Do not run dry (damages fuel pump).

## General Rules
- Notice colour: Red = Stop, Amber = Attend soon.
- Multiple red lights or red + noise/smoke/power loss = Stop and call tow.
- Tell service advisor which light is on and its colour.
- Do NOT ignore red lights or disconnect battery to clear codes.
