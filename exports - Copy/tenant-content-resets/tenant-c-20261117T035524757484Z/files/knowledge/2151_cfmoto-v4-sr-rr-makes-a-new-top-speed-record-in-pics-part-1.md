The V4 SR-RR is a superbike from CFMoto, that clocked an impressive top speed of 315.82kmph at the Shangrao proving ground in China.
18 Jun, 2026
Pranav Rawat
The bike was first unveiled at EICMA 2025, and is still in the testing phase.
Its top speed is now said to be a new record for a Chinese production engine.
The CFMoto V4 SR-RR uses a 997cc V4 engine, producing 210PS and 115Nm.
It is easily one of the most striking looking superbikes in the world, with aggressive styling, and sharp body panels.
The bike also gets quite large winglets at the front, which improves aerodynamics, and also improves the overall stance of the bike.
It also gets a single sided swing arm, which completes its overall aesthetic package.
It gets a lightweight aluminium frame, with the engine being a stressed member, and runs on lightweight forged aluminium 17-inch wheels.
Other than this, the V4 SR-RR is also expected to come loaded with all the rider's aids, and safety features.
