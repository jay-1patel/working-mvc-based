## 4. Wear & Tear Parts - Typical Life
Some parts wear out with use and are not covered by the service schedule or warranty. The table below gives the typical life of common wear and tear parts and the signs that tell you a part needs attention. If you notice any of these signs between services, do not wait for the next service - call us on +91 98765 43211 and get the car checked.

| Part | Typical Life | Signs it needs attention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brake pads** | 30,000 - 40,000 km | Squealing sound while braking, longer stopping distance, brake warning light |
| **Battery** | 3 - 4 years | Slow cranking, dim headlights, repeated jump-starts |
| **Tyres** | 40,000 - 50,000 km or 5 years | Tread worn to the indicator marks, sidewall cracks, uneven wear, vibration at speed |
| **Clutch** | 60,000 - 80,000 km, depends on driving style | Clutch slipping, hard pedal, burning smell, difficulty in changing gears |
| **Wiper blades** | About 1 year | Streaks, chattering or smearing on the windshield |
| **Shock absorbers** | 60,000 - 80,000 km | Bouncy ride, nose-diving under braking, oil leakage on the strut |
| **AC gas (refrigerant)** | Top-up every 2 - 3 years | Weak cooling, warm air from the vents |
| **Bulbs (headlight and others)** | As needed | Any lamp not working - replace immediately for safety and legality |

## 5. If You Miss a Service
Missing a service does not harm the car in one day, but the damage builds up quietly. Old engine oil thickens and forms sludge, which blocks oil passages and wears the engine from inside. A clogged air filter reduces mileage and power. Old brake fluid absorbs moisture and can make braking weaker. Parts that would have been caught early in an inspection, like worn brake pads or a weak battery, can fail suddenly on the road.

There are money consequences too. Your car's warranty of 3 years or 1,00,000 km requires servicing on schedule at an authorised workshop, so skipped services can lead to a warranty claim being refused. A car with a complete service record also gets a better resale and exchange value, because buyers trust a car that was maintained on time.

## 6. Eco-Friendly Car Tips
Small habits make your car cleaner and cheaper to run. These tips reduce fuel use and pollution, and most of them also save you money.

*   **Keep tyres at the recommended pressure.** Under-inflated tyres burn more fuel, wear out faster and pollute more; check pressure every two weeks.
*   **Service your car on time.** A clean air filter, fresh engine oil and correct spark plugs help the engine burn fuel fully, which improves mileage and reduces emissions.
*   **Remove extra weight** from the boot and take off roof carriers and boxes when they are not needed. Extra weight and wind resistance both increase fuel use.
*   **Switch off the engine** at long signals and in long traffic jams instead of idling. A modern car uses more fuel idling for a minute than it uses to restart.
*   **Use the AC sensibly.** Park in shade where possible, and on a hot day, open the windows for the first minute of driving to let hot air out before switching the AC on.
*   **Plan your trips.** Combine short trips into one round, and avoid peak traffic where possible; one longer trip pollutes less than several cold short trips.
*   **Fill fuel only at trusted pumps.** Clean fuel keeps the injectors and fuel filter healthy and keeps emissions low.
