# AC Not Cooling

## Symptoms
- Blows normal or warm air.
- Weak cooling or intermittent operation.
- Bad smell from vents.

## Immediate Actions
1. Check settings: AC ON, Temp Cool, Fan High, Recirculate Mode.
2. Vent hot air: Open windows for 1 minute, then close and run AC.
3. If weak persists: Likely clogged cabin filter or low AC gas.

## Important Warnings
- **Do NOT** refill AC gas repeatedly without a leak check.
- **Do NOT** ignore bad smells (fungus/dirty filter).
- **If Engine Overheats:** Switch AC OFF immediately.

## Common Causes
- Low AC gas (leak).
- Clogged cabin filter.
- Faulty compressor or cooling fan.
- Dirty condenser.
