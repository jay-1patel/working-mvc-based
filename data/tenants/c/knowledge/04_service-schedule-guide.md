# Service schedule by purchase date

When a customer asks "Which service does my car need? I purchased it on ", ask for the purchase / delivery date and current odometer (km). Use whichever comes earlier between time since purchase and kilometres driven.

| When (from purchase / delivery) | Typical km | Service needed | What we do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| About 1 month | ~1,000 km | 1st free inspection / PDI follow-up | Fluid levels, tyre pressure, basic health check, software/recall notice if any |
| About 6 months | ~10,000 km | 1st periodic service | Engine oil + oil filter, inspection checklist, top-ups as needed |
| About 12 months | ~20,000 km | 2nd periodic service | Oil & filter, air filter check, brake & underbody inspection |
| About 18 months | ~30,000 km | 3rd periodic service | Oil & filter, cabin/air filter as due, suspension & battery check |
| About 24 months | ~40,000 km | 4th periodic / major check | Oil service + spark plugs / coolant / brake fluid as per km chart |
| Every 6 months after that | Every ~10,000 km | Ongoing periodic service | Repeat oil service; replace filters and wear parts when due |
| Any time after accident / warning light | — | Repair / diagnosis visit | Scan, estimate, denting-painting or mechanical repair as required |

**How to answer:** Calculate months since purchase date and compare with odometer. Example — purchased 15 Mar 2025, today 15 Nov 2025 (~8 months), odometer 12,000 km → due for 1st periodic service (10,000 km / 6 months already passed). If only purchase date is known, use the time column and remind the customer to share km at booking. Always confirm a written estimate before work.
