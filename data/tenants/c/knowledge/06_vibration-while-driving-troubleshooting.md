# Steering or Seat Vibration

## Symptoms
- Steering wheel vibrates at certain speeds (often 60-100 km/h).
- Whole car or seat shakes.
- Started after hitting a pothole.

## Common Causes
- Wheels need balancing.
- Wheel alignment is out.
- Damaged tyre (bulge in sidewall).
- Bent rim from pothole impact.

## What To Do
1. Slow down. Reduce speed until vibration is mild.
2. Stop safely and inspect all four tyres for bulges/cuts, bent rims, and loose nuts.
3. If damaged/bent: Change to spare wheel or call for help.
4. If looks fine: Drive carefully to workshop for balancing and alignment check.

## Warning
- Do NOT keep driving at high speed with strong vibration.
- Do NOT ignore vibration after a pothole hit; hidden damage can cause blowouts later.
