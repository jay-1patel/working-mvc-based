Clear your calendars and charge your phones, because the Auto Expo is coming back. The third edition of the Bharat Mobility Global Expo will take over New Delhi from February 4 to 9, 2027, and it’s shaping up to be the biggest yet. Expect over 1,750 exhibitors, 11 shows, around 10 lakh visitors, and enough new metal to keep car and bike fans busy for days.

This year’s theme is “Smarter Mobility For a Better Tomorrow”, but we know you’re coming for the shiny new launches. Here’s everything you need to know before you plan your visit.

The expo runs from February 4 to 9, 2027. Day one is for media and industry folks only, so the public can visit from February 5. Pick your day wisely, because weekends at the Expo are famously busy.
