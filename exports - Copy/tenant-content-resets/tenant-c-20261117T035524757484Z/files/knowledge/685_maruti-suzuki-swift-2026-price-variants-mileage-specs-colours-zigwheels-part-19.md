## Swift Key Highlights

**Variants:**
Maruti Suzuki offers the 2024 Swift in 5 broad trims: LXi, Vxi, VxI (O), Zxi, and ZXi+. The CNG variant is available in 3 trims: Vxi, Vxi (O) and Zxi.
It can be had with 6 monotone and 3 dual-tone colour options: Sizzling Red, Luster Blue, Novel Orange, Magma Grey, Splendid Silver, and Arctic White. The Red, Blue, and White hues can be chosen with a dual-tone option with a black roof.
