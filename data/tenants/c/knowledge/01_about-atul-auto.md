# About Atul Auto

Driving Excellence Since the 1970s.

Atul Auto is a renowned commercial vehicle manufacturer, remaining at the forefront of the automotive industry by providing an exceptional range of three-wheeled vehicles. By understanding the unique challenges faced by rural communities, Atul Auto designs vehicles that not only meet practical needs but also foster a sense of connection and support.

Our commitment to uplifting the daily lives of our community ensures that each journey is a step towards a better future.
