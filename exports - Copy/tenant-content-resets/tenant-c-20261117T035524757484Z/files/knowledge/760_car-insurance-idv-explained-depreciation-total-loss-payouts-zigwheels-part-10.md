This is where picking the right Car Insurance policy helps. A comprehensive plan that lets you customise IDV, add accessories, and stack covers such as Return to Invoice and zero depreciation puts you in control of what you'll actually recover; for instance, it lets owners set their IDV within the permitted range online and offers cashless repairs at 10,000+ network garages, so a partial-damage claim doesn't drain your bank account while the car is being fixed.

Here's something few owners realise: the total-loss trigger is a percentage of IDV, so two owners of the same car can walk away from the same crash with completely different outcomes. The repair bill doesn't change. Only the IDV does.
