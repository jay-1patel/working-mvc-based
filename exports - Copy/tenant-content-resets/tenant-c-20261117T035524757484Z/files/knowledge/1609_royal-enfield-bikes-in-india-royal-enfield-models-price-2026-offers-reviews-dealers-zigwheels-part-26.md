## Which Royal Enfield to buy?

**Bear 650:** A retro road scrambler inspired by the Big Bear Run, the Bear 650 pairs the 648cc parallel-twin platform with a bench seat and beefier suspension. It slots in as a more rugged, adventure-styled sibling to the Super Meteor 650.

**Adventure:**

**Himalayan 450:** A truly go-anywhere motorcycle, the Himalayan 450 is an adventure tourer that’s capable of tackling rough roads and long highway rides.

**Scram 411:** Based on the now discontinued Himalayan 411, the Scram 411 blends the capabilities of a scrambler with urban practicality. Powered by a 411cc single-cylinder engine, it offers balanced performance for both city commutes and weekend adventures.

**Scram 440:** The successor to the Scram 411, now built on the Himalayan 450's 452cc platform for stronger performance. It keeps the scrambler-meets-commuter formula while gaining the newer engine and electronics.
