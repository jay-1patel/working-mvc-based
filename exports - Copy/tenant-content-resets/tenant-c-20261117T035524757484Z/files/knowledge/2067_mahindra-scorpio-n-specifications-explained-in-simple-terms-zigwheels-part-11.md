## 4XPLOR And BLD: Your Off-Road Assistants

Here’s the good news for first-time off-roaders: the Scorpio-N 4x4 doesn’t need an expert behind the wheel. Two smart systems take care of most of the hard work.

- **4XPLOR terrain modes:** Turn a dial to choose Normal, Snow, Mud & Ruts or Sand. The Scorpio-N then adjusts how it delivers power and manages wheelspin for that surface. You pick where you’re going, and it works out how to get there.
- **Brake Locking Differential (BLD):** Off-road, a common way to get stuck is when one wheel loses grip, either hanging in the air or spinning in mud. Most of the engine’s power then goes to that free wheel. BLD automatically brakes the spinning wheel, which sends power back to the wheels that still have traction.
- **Adventure Statistics:** The 4WD variants also show useful off-road information in the cabin, so you can see what the SUV is doing on the trail.
