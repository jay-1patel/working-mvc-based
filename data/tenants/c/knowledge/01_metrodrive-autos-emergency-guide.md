# MetroDrive Autos Emergency Guide

**Contact:** +91 98250 22011

## Immediate Actions
- **Red Oil Light / Heavy Smoke:** Stop immediately. Do not drive. Arrange tow.
- **Engine Fire:** Close bonnet, evacuate, call emergency services then MetroDrive.
- **Brake Failure:** Use handbrake gradually, look for escape route.

## General Maintenance Tips
- Check tyre pressures weekly.
- Monitor oil levels regularly.
- Keep service records updated.
- Address minor noises early to prevent major repairs.
