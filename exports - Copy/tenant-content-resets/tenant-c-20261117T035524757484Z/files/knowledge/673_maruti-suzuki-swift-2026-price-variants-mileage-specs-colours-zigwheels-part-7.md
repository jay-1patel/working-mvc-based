## Swift Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Cool and sporty looking
- New engine promises more efficiency
- Larger touch screen and wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay
- 6 airbags, hill-hold assist and other safety features are standard

**Could be Better**

- Still not as premium on the inside as rivals like the Grand i10 Nios
- Priced close to larger and more premium rivals like the Hyundai i20 and stable mate, the Baleno

**Expert's Conclusion**

The new Swift is promising a lot. More features, more safety and more efficiency but also at a higher asking price.
