## Space & Practicality

- The XEV 9e puts its size to good use offering great cabin space even for tall users
- Even with the sloping roof line, there is good headroom for users up to or a little over 6ft in height
- Thanks to the flat floor, seating 3 occupants at the rear is also comfortable so long as the users are of an average build
- Rear seat users can adjust the backrest angle and the front passenger’s seat can be adjusted from the rear seat using a lever too
- The door pockets are large enough to accommodate big bottles and there’s a large space between the front passengers to store more items
- Without the parcel cover in place, there’s enough room to fit 2 large suitcases into the boot
- The boot space is rated at 663 litres but with the sloping roofline, there’s only space for one full-sized suitcase with the roller-type parcel cover in use
- The XUV700, a car that’s a little smaller in size than the XEV 9e can seat 7 while this is a 5 seater only with a boot that’s isn’t bigger either
