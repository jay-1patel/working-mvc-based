## About Bajaj

November 2025, the Bajaj-KTM relationship entered a new phase when Bajaj Auto, through its subsidiary Bajaj Auto International completed a €800 million transaction to take full control of Pierer Bajaj AG, effectively becoming the majority owner of KTM's parent, Pierer Mobility AG, with a stake of around 74.9%. The deal closed on November 18, 2025, after Bajaj received all required regulatory approvals, with Pierer Bajaj AG since being renamed Bajaj Auto International Holdings AG. This made KTM, Husqvarna and GasGas step-down subsidiaries of Bajaj Auto, and the acquisition was highlighted as a defining development in Bajaj's FY2026 results, alongside record export volumes and its Chetak electric scooter crossing 700,000 cumulative units sold.
