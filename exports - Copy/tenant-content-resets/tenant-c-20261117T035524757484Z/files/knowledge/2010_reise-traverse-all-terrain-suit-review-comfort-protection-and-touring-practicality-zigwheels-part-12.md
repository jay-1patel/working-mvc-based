## Water Resistance

Weather is another part of adventure riding that you can't always plan for. You could leave home in sunshine and find yourself riding through rain a few hours later.

The Traverse uses water-resistant construction designed to deal with changing weather conditions. To demonstrate how the suit handles exposure to water, we took it to a car washing centre and gave it a thorough soaking.

The test focused on the outer material, zips, closures and the connection between the jacket and pants. After the wash, we inspected the suit and checked the inside for signs of water ingress.

It's worth making a distinction here. A pressure wash is a useful visual demonstration, but it doesn't establish a formal waterproof rating or guarantee that the suit will keep you dry in every condition. The result also depends on how the garment is constructed, how the closures are secured and how long it's exposed to water.
