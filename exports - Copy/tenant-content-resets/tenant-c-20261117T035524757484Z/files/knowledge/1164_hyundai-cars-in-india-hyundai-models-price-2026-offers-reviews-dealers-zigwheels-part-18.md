## About Hyundai

Electric Vehicle: The Creta Electric and Ioniq 5 are Hyundai's offerings in the Indian EV market. The Creta Electric rivals the Tata Nexon EV, while the Ioniq 5 competes with premium EVs like the BYD Atto 3.

Sub-compact SUV: The Hyundai Venue represents Hyundai in the sub-4 metre SUV segment. It goes up against the likes of the Maruti Brezza and Kia Sonet.

Micro SUV: The Hyundai Exter competes in the entry-level SUV space, rivalling the Tata Punch and Maruti Fronx.

Compact SUV: Hyundai has one of the best selling compact SUVs in its portfolio - the Hyundai Creta. It faces stiff competition from rivals like the Kia Seltos, Maruti Grand Vitara, and the Skoda Kushaq.

Mid-size SUV: Hyundai offers the Tucson as a 5-seater mid-size SUV that goes head to head with the likes of the Jeep Compass. It also offers the Creta-based Hyundai Alcazar SUV available in 6 or 7 seater configurations. It rivals the likes of Tata Safari as well as the Mahindra XUV700.
