# Eco Driving & Car Care

Keep your car efficient, cleaner, and longer-lasting with these tips:

## Driving Habits
- **Warm up gently:** Avoid high RPM for the first 1–2 km after a cold start.
- **Smooth driving:** Smooth throttle and early upshifts save fuel; hard acceleration and late braking waste it.
- **Tyre Pressure:** Keep tyre pressure at the door-sticker PSI — under-inflated tyres cut mileage and raise wear.
- **Reduce Weight:** Remove unused roof racks and heavy boot clutter; extra weight and drag reduce economy.
- **AC Usage:** Use AC wisely — recirculate after cabin cools; park in shade when possible.
- **Idling:** Idle less than 30–60 seconds when waiting; switch off if safe and legal for longer stops.
- **Route Planning:** Plan routes to avoid peak congestion when you can; steady 40–60 km/h city speeds are kinder on fuel.

## Maintenance
- **Service Schedule:** Follow the service schedule on time — clean oil, filters and plugs keep combustion efficient.
- **Genuine Parts:** Prefer the recommended grade of engine oil and genuine filters for best economy and emissions.
- **Monthly Checks:** Check PUC, battery terminals and coolant level monthly; fix warning lights promptly.
- **Washing:** Wash with pH-neutral shampoo; avoid harsh chemicals on paint and plastic trims.
- **Long Trips:** Check tyre pressure cold, top up washer fluid, and carry a basic tool/kit.
