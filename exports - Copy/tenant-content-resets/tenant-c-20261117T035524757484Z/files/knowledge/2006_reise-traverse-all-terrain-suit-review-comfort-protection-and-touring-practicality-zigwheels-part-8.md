## The Armour Test

The Traverse uses CE Level 2 D3O protection across several major impact zones.

The jacket gets D3O T5 EVO PRO X Level 2 shoulder and elbow protectors, along with a D3O Viper Air Level 2 back protector and D3O CP2 chest protectors. The pants use D3O T5 EVO PRO X Level 2 protectors at the knees and hips.

That's a fairly comprehensive spread of armour, covering the areas you'd expect to need protection in a fall.

For our test, we took a slightly less conventional approach. We dressed a host in the Traverse and put him through the Protection Detail Lab's very scientific Office Ambush Test, using plastic baseball bats to demonstrate the locations of the armour.

And before you try this at home, remember that we're willing to put our colleague in harm's way so you don't have to.
