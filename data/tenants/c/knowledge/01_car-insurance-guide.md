# Car Insurance Guide

## Types of Insurance
*   **Third-Party Insurance:** Mandatory by law. Covers damage/injury to others and their property. Does NOT cover your own car.
*   **Comprehensive Insurance:** Recommended. Covers third-party liabilities PLUS damage to your own car from accidents, theft, fire, and natural calamities.

## Popular Add-ons
*   **Zero Depreciation:** Insurer pays full cost of replaced parts without depreciation deduction.
*   **Engine Protect:** Covers engine damage caused by water entering the engine.
*   **Return to Invoice:** If the car is stolen or damaged beyond repair, you get the full invoice value.
*   **Roadside Assistance:** 24x7 help for breakdowns, flat tyres, battery jump-start, and towing.

## No Claim Bonus (NCB)
Discount on renewal premium for claim-free years. Starts at 20% after the first year and goes up to 50% after five years. Transferable between insurers/cars.

## Claims Process
1.  Inform insurer and MetroDrive service team immediately.
2.  Bring car (or arrange towing) to our workshop.
3.  Carry Driving Licence, RC, and Policy Copy.
4.  Surveyor inspects the car.
5.  Cashless claims available with leading insurers; insurer pays workshop directly.
