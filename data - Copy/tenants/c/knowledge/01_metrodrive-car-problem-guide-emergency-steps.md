# MetroDrive Car Problem Guide

## General First Steps for Any Problem
Whatever the problem — brakes, steering, smoke, burst tyre — follow these steps:
1. Stay calm. Do not panic.
2. Slow down gradually. Lift foot off accelerator.
3. Switch ON hazard lights.
4. Move to left side or safe open space.
5. Stop fully, pull handbrake, switch engine OFF.
6. If hurt/fire, call 112 first.
7. Get out only when safe.

**Note:** Never try any check or repair while the car is still moving.

## Problem 1: Brake Failure
### Symptoms
- Brake pedal goes to floor.
- Car does not slow down.
- Pedal feels loose/no pressure.

### Immediate Actions
1. Stay calm, keep hands on wheel.
2. Pump brake pedal fast/repeatedly.
3. Use engine braking (lower gear).
4. Switch ON hazard lights.
5. Pull handbrake slowly/gradually.
6. Steer to safe space.
7. If crash certain, aim for soft objects (bushes, uphill).

### Do NOT Do
- Do NOT switch engine off while moving.
- Do NOT drive again after this happens.
- Do NOT pour water on hot brakes.

### Causes
- Brake fluid leak.
- Broken brake pipe/hose.
- Master cylinder failure.
- Worn-out parts.

### Help
Call MetroDrive Service: +91 98765 43211. Do not drive; we arrange tow.

## Problem 2: Brake Warning Signs
### Symptoms
- Soft/spongy pedal.
- Squealing/grinding noise.
- Pulling left/right.
- Brake warning light ON.
- Longer stopping distance.

### Actions
1. Slow down, drive gently.
2. Leave extra distance.
3. Test brakes gently in safe place.
4. If symptoms persist, stop driving and call for help.
5. Otherwise, visit workshop same-day.
