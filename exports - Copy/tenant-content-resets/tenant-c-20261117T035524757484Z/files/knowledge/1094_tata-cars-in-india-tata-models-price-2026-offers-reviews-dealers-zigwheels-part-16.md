## About Tata

**History**

Part of the gigantic Tata group founded by Jamsetji Tata in 1868, Tata Motors is one of the leading Indian car manufacturers. Established in 1945, Tata Motors' diverse portfolio includes an extensive range of cars, sport utility vehicles, trucks, buses and defence vehicles. Jaguar and Land Rover; the two prestigious British car brands were acquired by Tata back in 2008.

Tata launched the Tata Indica in 1998; its first passenger car in India, which paved the way for the future models to come. Tata (Telco back then) was also responsible for bringing the three-pointed star (Mercedes-Benz) to India in 1995.

**Product portfolio**

Hatchback: Tata's hatchbacks' portfolio ranges from the Tiago and Tiago EV to the Altroz.

Micro SUV: Tata offers the Punch and Punch EV as entry-level micro SUVs. It takes on the Mahindra Exter and Maruti Fronx but due to its price, it also rivals the Nissan Magnite and Renault Kiger.
