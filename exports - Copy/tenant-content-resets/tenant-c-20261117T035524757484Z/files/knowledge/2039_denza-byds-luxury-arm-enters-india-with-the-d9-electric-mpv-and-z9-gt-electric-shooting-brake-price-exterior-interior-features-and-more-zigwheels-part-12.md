## Features

The equipment list includes a triple-screen setup: a 17.3-inch central touchscreen, a 13.2-inch passenger screen and a 13.2-inch digital driver’s display. You also get powered seats with ventilation, heating and massage functions, an AR head-up display, four-zone climate control, connected car tech and a dual-pane sunroof.

Other features include a 20-speaker Devialet sound system, powered doors with soft-close, a powered tailgate and auto park assist.

Safety amenities include an ADAS suite, nine airbags, ISOFIX child seat mounts, front and rear parking sensors, a tyre-pressure monitoring system (TPMS), traction control (TC), electronic stability control (ESC), and a 360-degree camera.
