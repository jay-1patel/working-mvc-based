## The Gearboxes: Six Speeds, Cleanly Done

Both engines come with a 6-speed manual or a 6-speed torque converter automatic. Both gearboxes use a shift-by-cable setup.

**What it means:**

- **Shift-by-cable:** On older SUVs, a rigid metal rod linked the gear lever to the gearbox, so engine vibration came straight through into your hand. The Scorpio-N uses cables instead, which keeps the lever separate from the engine’s movement. Every shift feels cleaner, and the lever stays calm at idle.
- **Torque converter automatic:** This is a conventional automatic that is smooth in traffic and suited to an SUV’s heavy-duty work. It also gets a torque boost on both engines: 400 Nm on the diesel and 380 Nm on the petrol.

**Choose your mood:** Diesel versions get three drive modes.

- **Zip:** Soft and easy-going, for stop-and-go traffic.
- **Zap:** The balanced everyday setting.
- **Zoom:** Sharper throttle response for when you want a more engaging drive.
