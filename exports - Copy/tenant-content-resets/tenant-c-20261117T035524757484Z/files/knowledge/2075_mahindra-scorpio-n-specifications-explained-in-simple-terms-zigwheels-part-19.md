## Seating: Six Or Seven, Your Choice

A Mahindra SUV has always been built to carry the whole family, and the Scorpio-N gives you two ways to do it.

- **7-seater:** Standard across the range, with a bench in the second row. The third row folds and tumbles when you need more luggage space.
- **6-seater:** Offered on the top Z8L. It replaces the second-row bench with captain seats, so the middle-row passengers get more comfort and easier access to the third row.
- **6-way powered driver seat:** On the Z8T and Z8L, adjusting your driving position is done at the press of a button.
- **Ventilated front seats:** Also on the Z8T and Z8L, they keep you cool through hot Indian summers.
