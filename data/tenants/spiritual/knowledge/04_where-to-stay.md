# Stay Options Near the Temple

## Temple Dharamshala (Divine Guide Yatri Niwas)
*   **Rooms:** 40 rooms — non-AC Rs. 500/night, AC Rs. 1,100/night (2–3 persons per room)
*   **Distance:** 200 metres from the temple, same lane
*   **Booking:** Temple office or seva desk (+91 98765 43211); up to 30 days ahead
*   **Check-in / out:** 10:00 AM / 9:00 AM
*   **Rules:** Photo ID of every adult is required. Rooms are for devotees only — no parties, no alcohol, no non-vegetarian food on the premises. Maximum stay 3 nights (5 in Shravan with office permission).

## Nearby Options (indicative)
*   **Budget guest houses, Har Ki Pauri area:** 300–700 m, Rs. 700–1,500 / night
*   **Mid-range hotels, Railway Road:** 1–2 km, Rs. 1,800–3,500 / night
*   **Riverside resorts, Bhoopatwala:** 3–4 km, Rs. 3,500 onwards / night

**Good to know:**
*   Rooms fill up first on weekends, Purnima, Ekadashi and in Shravan — book the dharamshala at least 2 weeks ahead for those days.
*   Free cloakroom and locker facility is available at the temple for devotees who arrive before check-in (6:00 AM – 9:00 PM, token system).
*   Families arriving by night trains can rest in the Yatri Vishram Hall (Rs. 100 per person, mattresses provided) until rooms are ready.
