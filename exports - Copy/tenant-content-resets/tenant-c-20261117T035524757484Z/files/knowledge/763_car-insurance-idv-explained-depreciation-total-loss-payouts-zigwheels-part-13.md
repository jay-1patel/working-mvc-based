| Rubber, nylon and plastic parts | 50 percent |
| Fibreglass components | 30 percent |
| Glass | Nil |
| Paint | 50 percent on material cost of painting |

Now look at a modern car. Bumpers, cladding, grilles, and headlamp housings are mostly plastic so that a big frontal hit can trigger a 50 percent deduction on a large chunk of the bill. Insurance gives the example of a Rs 20,000 repair where Rs 6,000 vanishes to depreciation, leaving a Rs 14,000 payout without cover versus the full Rs 20,000 with a zero depreciation add-on. Scale that up to a major-damage bill running into lakhs, and the shortfall becomes serious money.
