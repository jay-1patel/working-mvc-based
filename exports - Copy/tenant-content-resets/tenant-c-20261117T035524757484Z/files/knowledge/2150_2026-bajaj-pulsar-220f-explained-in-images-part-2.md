Powering the bike is a 220cc oil-cooled engine producing 20.9PS and 18.6Nm, paired with a 5-speed gearbox.

The bike is equipped with telescopic forks and twin shock absorbers at the rear, along with dual disc brakes.

The bike gets a digital instrument console with Bluetooth connectivity, all-LED lighting and a USB charging port, but misses out on a gear position indicator.
