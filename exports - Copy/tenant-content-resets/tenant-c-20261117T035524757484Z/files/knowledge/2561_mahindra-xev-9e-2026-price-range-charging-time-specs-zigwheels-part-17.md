## Performance & Range

**Will I Like Driving The XEV 9e?**

- The XEV 9e is offered with two battery packs - 59kWh or 79kWh
- Offered only with rear-wheel drive, the motor makes 230PS with the smaller battery and 285PS with the bigger battery. Both versions offer 380Nm of torque
- The XEV 9e is both quick and smooth to drive, with gentle power delivery for city commutes and punch for high speed overtaking
- 0-100kmph takes a claimed 6.8 seconds only!
- One-pedal driving and regen calibration is smooth. First time electric car buyers can get used to the experience quickly
