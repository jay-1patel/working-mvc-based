## About Bajaj

**Latest Update on Bajaj Auto**

2026 has been a busy year for Bajaj Auto. Since November 2025, the company has updated seven motorcycles, including the latest Pulsar 150 and Pulsar 125. Bajaj is also expected to introduce eight more motorcycles over the next few months, strengthening its lineup across the original Pulsar range, the NS series and the newer N series. Reports also suggest the company is working on a new sub-brand for motorcycles in the 125cc to 250cc segment. Alongside its petrol-powered models, Bajaj recently launched the Chetak C25 electric scooter with a claimed range of 113km and is developing a modular electric motorcycle platform for its upcoming EVs.

**Origins**

Bajaj Auto Limited is an Indian multinational two-wheeler and three-wheeler manufacturing company that was founded in 1945. The company was initially established as a trading company, importing and selling Vespa scooters in India under license from the Italian company Piaggio.
