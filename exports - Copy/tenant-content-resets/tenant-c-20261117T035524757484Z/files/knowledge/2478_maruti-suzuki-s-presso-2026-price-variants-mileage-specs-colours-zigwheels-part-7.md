## S-Presso Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Quirky looks and interiors will stand out for sure
- Interiors are spacious
- Light controls, slick shifting gearbox and tractable BS6 compliant engine make it great to drive in the city

**Could be Better**

- Pricey for the features on offer and even more expensive to customise
- Quirky looks may not be everyone's cup of coffee

**Expert's Conclusion**

It's easy to drive nature and spacious interiors will make the S-Presso appealing to new drivers. We just wish that it was better equipped and better priced.
