## Suspension: Where The Comfort Comes From

- **Double wishbone (front):** Each front wheel is held in place by two arms, one above the other. This keeps the tyre flat against the road as the suspension moves, so you get better grip and more precise steering on bumpy roads.
- **Coil-over shocks:** The spring and shock absorber are combined into one compact unit.
- **Pentalink (rear):** Five links hold the rear axle in place, which controls its movement more precisely than simpler designs. On broken roads, the rear stays more stable and composed.
- **Watt’s linkage:** An extra linkage that stops the rear axle from shifting sideways as it moves up and down. You feel it as a steadier rear end through corners and over uneven surfaces.
- **Frequency Dependent Damping (FDD):** This is clever shock absorber tech. FDD reacts differently to small, quick bumps, such as expansion joints and rough patches, than to big, slow movements like potholes and speed breakers.
