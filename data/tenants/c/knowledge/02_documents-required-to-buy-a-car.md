# Documents Needed to Buy a Car

## Individual Buyer
*   Aadhaar card
*   PAN card
*   Address proof (utility bill, passport, or rent agreement)
*   Passport-size photographs
*   Driving licence (also required for test drive)

## For Car Loans
*   **Salaried:** Salary slips (last 3 months), Form 16, Bank statement (last 6 months)
*   **Self-employed:** Income Tax Returns (last 2 years), Bank statement (last 6 months)

## For Car Exchange
*   RC (registration certificate) of the old car
*   Insurance copy of the old car
*   Service history of the old car (if available)
*   NOC from the bank (if a loan is still running on the old car)

*Note: Our sales team checks all documents at the time of booking (booking amount Rs. 11,000, fully refundable until delivery) and helps complete any missing paperwork.*
