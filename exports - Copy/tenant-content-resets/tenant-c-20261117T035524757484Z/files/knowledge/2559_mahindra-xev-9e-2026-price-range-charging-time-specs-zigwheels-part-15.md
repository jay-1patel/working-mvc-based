## Battery & Charging

**Talk About Charging The XEV 9e**

- Using a 175kW charger, the 79kWh battery can go from 20-80 percent charge in 20 minutes
- Using a 140kW charger, the 59kWh battery can go from 20-80 percent charge in 20 minutes
- With the optional 11kW AC fast charger, the XEV 9e can be charged from 0-100 percent in 6-8 hours (59-79kWh batteries respectively)
- With the optional 7kW AC fast charger, the XEV 9e can be charged from 0-100 percent in 9-12 hours (59-79kWh batteries respectively)
- Claimed range for the 79kWh battery pack stands at 682km though it should manage 500-550km in real-world conditions
- Claimed range for the 59kWh battery pack stands at 535km though it should manage 380-450km in real-world conditions
- While charging options for the car are vast, the public charging infrastructure is still often inconsistent and not dependable in many locations
