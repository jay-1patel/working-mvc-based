## Ventilation

Heat is another thing you have to deal with when riding in India. A suit that works well on a cool morning can become uncomfortable once the temperature climbs, particularly when you're moving slowly or spending time in traffic.

The Traverse uses Reise's AirMorph ventilation system, which lets you adjust airflow through the jacket depending on the conditions.

We demonstrated this using a simple fan test, with lightweight ribbons around the ventilation openings to make the airflow visible. It's a basic way of showing how air moves around the suit when the vents are opened.

Of course, this isn't a test of how cool the jacket will feel on a hot afternoon. That depends on the temperature, humidity, riding speed and how much airflow actually reaches the rider. But it does help demonstrate the ventilation system and how it works.
