## FRONX Pros and Cons

**Good Things**

- Mini Grand Vitara styling
- Cabin offers enough space for four six-footers
- Supple ride quality
- One-litre turbo-petrol engine offers all-round drivability and performance

**Could be Better**

- Lacks niceties like sunroof and ventilated seats
- Sloping roofline eats into rear headroom
- Rear cabin lacks adequate storage spaces

**Expert's Conclusion**

The Fronx offers a good all around balance with its good looks, likeable cabin and potent powertrain options. It’s one of the perfect all rounders out there.
