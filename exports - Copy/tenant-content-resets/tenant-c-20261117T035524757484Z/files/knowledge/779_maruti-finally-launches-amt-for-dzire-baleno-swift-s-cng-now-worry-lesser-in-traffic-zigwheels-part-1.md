Maruti has launched the new, much-awaited AMT powertrain options for the Baleno, Swift and Dzire S-CNG. With growing demand for CNG-powered vehicles, it only makes sense to provide more options in the CNG space, and the added convenience of an automatic helps with driving in traffic and keeps running costs low. This new powertrain option is available only for cars which are powered by Maruti’s new Z12E 1.2-litre naturally aspirated engine.

Let's explore the new CNG AMT variant of Maruti:

The Baleno S-CNG AMT is available from Rs 8.32 lakh (ex-showroom) onwards.

| **Variant** | **Price** |
| Delta CNG AMT | Rs 8.32 lakh |
| Zeta CNG AMT | Rs 9.32 lakh |

The recently facelifted Baleno S-CNG now gets the added flexibility of AMT, which adds extra convenience of driving over the manual gearbox variants.

The Swift S-CNG with AMT is available from Rs 7.92 lakh (ex-showroom) onwards.
