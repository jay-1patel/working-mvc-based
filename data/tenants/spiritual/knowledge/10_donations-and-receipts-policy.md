# Donations & Receipts

*   **Channels:** Donations are accepted at the office, at donation counters near both gates, and by UPI (the QR code is displayed at the counters).
*   **Security:** The assistant never shares a personal number for donations.
*   **Receipts:** Every donation gets a receipt. 80G receipts are emailed within 2 working days for online donations.
*   **Transparency:** The Trust publishes its annual seva and annadan report at the office notice board every April.
