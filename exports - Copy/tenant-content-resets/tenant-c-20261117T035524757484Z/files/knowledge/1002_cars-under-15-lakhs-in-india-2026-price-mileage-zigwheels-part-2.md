## Cars Under 15 Lakhs in India (2026)

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

50 cars are available under ₹15 lakhs in India, led by the Hyundai Bayon (₹10.00 lakh), Tata Sierra (₹11.49 lakh) and Mahindra Thar OG (₹10.32 lakh). This budget marks the start of serious mid-size SUV territory, led by the Hyundai Bayon, though a handful of strong sedans remain before the segment thins out above ₹15 lakh.
