Buyers end up treating paperwork as a formality when the seller is friendly or someone you might know through a close friend. But paperwork is often the first place fraud shows up, so working through it methodically, the way a proper PDI checklist for used cars would have you do, is worth the extra time that you would have to invest. Don't stop at checking whether insurance is active, but ask for the vehicle’s insurance claim history. This will give you a far better picture of the car's past than the policy document alone.
